Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

PNPtv respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in strict compliance with the GDPR (Europe), CCPA/CPRA (USA), and Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012 (Colombia — Habeas Data).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Username, profile data, email, and authentication data from third-party login providers.
  • Usage Data: IP address, device information, browser type, and interaction logs.
  • Message Content: Direct messages are scanned automatically for safety purposes (see Section 7).
  • Image Fingerprints: Digital fingerprints (hashes) generated from uploaded photos and videos to detect content from banned members. Fingerprints do not analyze faces (see Section 7).
  • Facial Data (optional): Collected only if you consent to face matching, which is required to upload photos and videos or go live (see Section 7).
  • Payment Data: Processed securely by third-party providers (e.g., ePayco, Meru). We do not store full credit card information.
  • Verification Data: Government-issued ID or biometric data specifically requested for age and 2257 compliance (stored securely and temporarily as required by law).

2. Legal Basis & How We Use Your Information

We process your data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the Platform and process transactions.
  • Legitimate Interest: To prevent fraud, improve services, and maintain platform security.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with tax, age-verification, and criminal prevention laws.
  • Consent: For marketing communications, optional cookies, and face matching (biometric data).

3. Data Sharing & International Transfers

We may share data with payment processors, hosting providers, and legal authorities when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence (including Colombia and the USA). We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for international data transfers.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as your account is active, or as required by law (e.g., financial records, 2257 compliance data, and fraud prevention logs).

If an account is permanently banned, its profile and content are deleted, but we keep a minimal record of the identifiers needed to prevent the banned member from returning (see Section 7).

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following rights:

Europe (GDPR)

  • The right to access, rectify, and erase your data (Right to be Forgotten)
  • The right to restrict and object to processing
  • The right to data portability

Colombia (Ley 1581 de 2012 — Habeas Data)

  • The right to know, update, and rectify your personal data
  • The right to request proof of authorization
  • The right to revoke consent

USA (CCPA/CPRA)

  • California residents have the right to know what data is collected
  • The right to request deletion of personal data
  • The right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data

Note: PNPtv does not sell your data.

Requests to exercise these rights must be submitted to: legal@pnptv.app

6. Security & Cookies

We implement high-level technical and organizational measures to protect data, though no system is 100% secure.

We use cookies to maintain sessions and analyze usage. You may disable non-essential cookies via your browser or our cookie consent banner.

7. Automated Safety Scanning

To keep our community safe, PNPtv uses automated AI tools to scan content on the Platform. This includes posts, photos, videos, live streams, and direct messages. Content and message scanning is part of our Terms and Conditions and applies to all members.

How DM Scanning Works

  • Messages are scanned automatically by software. No human reads your messages as part of this process.
  • A message is sent to our safety team only if the system flags it as potentially dangerous or illegal, such as child sexual abuse material, trafficking, threats of violence, or the sale of illegal substances. A trained team member then reviews only the flagged content.
  • Messages are also reviewed if a member reports them to us.
  • We do not use your messages for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Image Fingerprinting (All Members)

  • When content from a banned member is removed, we create digital fingerprints (hashes) of it. Every new photo and video uploaded to PNPtv is checked against these fingerprints, and matching content is blocked.
  • Fingerprinting compares files, not faces. It does not analyze facial features and does not collect biometric data.

Face Matching (Optional, With Your Consent)

  • Face matching helps stop banned members from returning under new accounts. It compares the faces in your uploaded photos and videos against images of banned members and against publicly available images from the web.
  • Face matching analyzes facial features, which the law treats as sensitive biometric data. We use it only for safety and ban enforcement, never for marketing or any other purpose.
  • Face matching is optional. You are not required to consent. If you do not consent, you can still browse, chat, and use the community, but you will not be able to upload photos or videos or go live.
  • You can give or withdraw your consent at any time in your privacy settings. If you withdraw it, we delete your facial data and media upload features are paused.
  • When the system finds a likely match (a confidence score of 80% or higher), the account may be blocked. If you believe you were blocked by mistake, you can appeal at legal@pnptv.app.

Ban Enforcement & Linked Accounts

  • When a member is permanently banned for illegal content, their profile and content are deleted.
  • To prevent them from returning, we keep a minimal record of the identifiers needed to block them: IP addresses, device information, email addresses, linked accounts, and image fingerprints. We keep it only as long as necessary for safety and legal purposes.
  • We may use automated tools to identify other accounts or emails belonging to a banned member, and ban those as well.

Reporting to Authorities

Content involving child sexual abuse is reported to NCMEC and the relevant authorities, as required by law. We may also share flagged content with law enforcement when there is a serious threat to someone's safety.

Legal Basis

We carry out content scanning, message scanning, and image fingerprinting based on our legitimate interest in keeping the Platform safe and our legal obligations to prevent crime. We carry out face matching only with your explicit, prior, and informed consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

8. Modifications

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the updated version.
