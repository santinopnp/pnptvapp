Creator Casting — Welfare-First ATS

Core Principle

This is NOT a talent acquisition funnel. It is a welfare-first intake process that happens to result in creator onboarding. Every stage is designed to detect: coercion, financial desperation without alternatives, isolation, substance dependence that impairs consent, third-party control.

A platform that attracts creators who are NOT there by free choice will eventually destroy itself — legally, reputationally, and morally.

Creator Application Lifecycle

Stage 1 — Discovery

Track source: organic search, referral (which creator?), social, Telegram, word of mouth. Referral by another creator = highest-quality signal. Referral by unknown "manager" or "agent" = red flag.

Stage 2 — Initial Expression of Interest

Lightweight form: age, location, content type interest, Telegram handle. No photos, no content yet. Gate: must be 18+ (hard).

Stage 3 — Full Application

Full legal name + ID document, social media presence (optional), motivation, content plans, whether anyone helped complete the application, emergency contact.

Stage 4 — Document Verification

2257-compliant ID verification. Age confirmed. Identity cross-referenced. Zoho Sign: 2257 acknowledgment form signed electronically.

Stage 5 — Welfare Interview ← The Most Important Stage

30-minute video call. Conducted by Welfare & Safety Officer. Conversational, NOT interrogative. Assesses: voluntary participation, financial context, support network, substance use context, understanding of content permanence, third-party involvement.

Green flags: Clear autonomous motivation, informed about risks, support network present.
Soft flags: Financial pressure as motivator, limited support network, new to this type of work.
Hard disqualifications: Signs of third-party control, capacity-impairing substance dependence, does not understand content permanence.

Stage 6 — Trial Onboarding (30 days)

Full platform access. Weekly check-in from Creator Success Manager. Week 1: onboarding canvas. Week 2: first content welfare review. Week 4: trial review call.

Stage 7 — Active Creator

Monthly welfare check-in. Quarterly earnings review. Annual 2257 renewal reminder.

Stage 8 — Distress Flag Protocol

Triggers: no response for 14+ days, earnings drop 80%+ suddenly, abuse report, distress message. Response: personal outreach by Creator Success Manager. Options: extended break, harm reduction referral, full offboarding.

Stage 9 — Offboarding

Voluntary: content pause options explained. Deletion process clarified. Exit interview offered.
Involuntary: written reason, appeal process, 30-day archive period.
