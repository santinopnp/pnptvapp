Our Commitment to You — Complete Wellness & Development Guide

Version 1.0 — August 2026

“The question that founded us wasn’t ‘how do we make money from creators?’ It was ‘how do we make a creator’s career something they can be proud of — something that doesn’t cost them their health, and leaves them with something real when they decide to move on?’”

— PNPtv founding team

Before we begin: why this exists

Most adult content platforms operate like 19th-century factories: you provide the body, the time, and the risk; they keep most of the money and don’t care what happens to you afterward. No real onboarding, no dignified exit, no safety net.

PNPtv was built from inside that reality. Lex, one of our co-founders, lived it. Santino, the other, spent a decade recruiting and caring for corporate talent and asked himself: Why does nobody do for adult creators what companies do for executives?

The answer was PNPtv. And this document is that answer in concrete terms: what we do today, what we’re building, and how we measure whether we’re keeping our word.

The four pillars of the Creator Care Program

1. FAIR MONEY           → Your work has value, and what you earn is yours
2. BODY & MIND          → Your health isn't a cost — it's our investment
3. REAL GROWTH          → Skills, tools, education
4. COMMUNITY OF PEERS   → You're not alone in this

PILLAR 1 — Fair and transparent money

The split that doesn’t change

70% for you. 30% for the platform. Always. No fine print.

Here’s what that means in practice:

Income type Your share Note
Content sales (videos, photos) 70% 7-day hold before available
Fan subscriptions 70% 7-day hold
Livestream tips 100% No commission — it’s a direct gift
Private calls 70% 7-day hold

The 7-day hold exists to protect you: it prevents fraudulent payments from putting you in the negative. It’s not to hold your money.

How you get paid

You can withdraw your earnings in: - Bitcoin (BTC) - Dash (DASH) - USDT on Tron network (TRC-20) - USDT on Base (Ethereum L2 network) - Meru (for Colombia and LATAM — phone number or username)

Minimum for cashout: $50 USD. Minimum for the weekly batch: $100 USD (your balance accumulates if you don’t reach it — it’s never lost).

Your ledger — every dollar traceable

You have access to your complete transaction history: every tip received, every subscription, every call payment, every withdrawal. You don’t have to trust that 70% is 70% — you can verify it transaction by transaction.

(Feature in development — available Q4 2026)

PILLAR 2 — Body and mind

The 10% that comes back to you

Starting Q1 2027, PNPtv will allocate 10% of the net commission generated by each creator to an individual wellness fund. That fund is yours to spend on any service in this program. It’s not a performance bonus — it doesn’t depend on how much you earn, and it doesn’t disappear if you have a slow month. It’s an acknowledgment that your wellbeing is part of the business.

What can you spend it on?

Area What it covers
Mental health Psychology sessions, therapy, support groups
Sexual health Medical consultations, PrEP, PEP, STI testing, contraception
Financial literacy Savings courses, basic investing, crypto, taxes
English 1:1 or group classes with native speakers
Digital marketing Content strategy, SEO, social media, audience growth
Video editing Beginner courses (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere)
Photography Lighting, composition, editing for creators

(Providers for each category will be announced at fund launch — we’re prioritizing Latin American partners first)

Sober Body Program (launching soon)

For creators in our community who are in a process of reducing use or in recovery, Sober Body is a non-judgmental support program:

  • Private WhatsApp/Telegram group with peers who understand the PNP context — not an AA group, not religious, no judgment
  • Weekly virtual meetups moderated by a counselor with experience in LGBTQ+ communities and chemsex contexts
  • Optional individual check-ins — a trusted contact who asks how you’re doing
  • Harm reduction resources adapted to context: information on combinations, intervals, hydration, warning signs
  • Milestone recognition — the community celebrates your Sober Body days because they’re yours, not ours

This program is not mandatory and doesn’t affect your platform access. It’s an open door.

Self-Care Center (live now)

A section of the app dedicated only to you, with no ads, no algorithm, no work notifications:

  • Wellness Mode — activate a rest period for 1, 7, 30 days, or indefinitely. It has a deliberate 24-hour “cooling off” period: if in a difficult moment you want to deactivate it, you have 24 hours to think about it. This is not a bug — it’s intentional design to protect you from your own impulses.
  • Use Tracker — a private, judgment-free log of your use sessions. Only you see it. The data is for you, not for us.
  • Wellness Hangouts — peer support groups within the platform where you can talk with others
  • Cristina — your AI assistant available 24/7 for whatever you need: a health question, a payment question, a hard night

Individual counseling (Q4 2026)

Access to counseling sessions with professionals who know the industry. You don’t have to explain from scratch what PNP is, what being an adult creator means, what chemsex involves. They arrive knowing — you can go straight to what matters.

Available via: - Video call (private, in-app) - Text chat (async, for when you can’t talk)

PILLAR 3 — Real growth

Peer mentorship (Q4 2026)

Experienced PNPtv creators will be able to become Certified Mentors and support newcomers. The mentor receives compensation for their time (Ru$h tokens, convertible to cash). The new creator receives:

  • Personalized onboarding (not a PDF)
  • Profile and content strategy review
  • Support through the first 4 weeks
  • A real contact who’s been through what they’re going through

AI agents for creators (in development)

We’re building a set of AI tools that work for you, not for the platform:

  • Content description agent — generates titles and descriptions that sell, so you don’t have to write from scratch
  • Pricing strategy agent — analyzes your audience and suggests what price makes sense for each content type
  • Publishing calendar agent — tells you when your audience is most active and reminds you to post
  • Earnings analysis agent — explains in plain language what worked this month and why
  • Cristina (live now) — general support, questions, ticket management, reminders

All these agents work inside the app, in Spanish and English.

PILLAR 4 — Community of peers

Wellness hangouts (live now)

Private groups within the app where creators connect with each other — not as competition, but as community. There are themed hangouts: general wellness, recovery, finances, creative career.

Community safety

When a creator reports another, the system acts immediately: - The reported user is automatically blocked for the reporter - CSAM triggers automatic account suspension - Protected categories: harassment, hate, non-consent, self-harm

Slack support network (active)

Every active creator has a private channel in PNPtv’s Slack (#ext-[handle]) where the PNPtv team communicates with them directly. This isn’t a broadcast channel — it’s two-way communication.

Recognition and gamification (available)

Accumulated Sober Body days, community badges, Wall of Fame, points system — because small wins deserve public recognition.

Protections that already exist (not promises — code in production today)

This isn’t what we’re going to do. It’s what already exists in the platform right now:

Identity and privacy - AI age verification (Azure Face API / Face++) — the photo is discarded immediately after analysis, never stored - § 2257 verification with Persona.com integration for government ID - 100% passwordless authentication (passkeys + magic links — phishing-resistant by design) - Right to erasure: full account deletion with one command, no need to talk to anyone - Location fuzzing: your position in Nearby is never exact — always shifted 100–500m randomly

Payments - Crypto payments in (NowPayments) — irreversible, no chargebacks - Cashout in 5 channels (BTC, DASH, USDT TRC-20, USDT Base, Meru) - AES-256 encryption of all payment data - Real-time fraud detection (transaction velocity, geographic anomalies)

Safety and moderation - Reporting system with 8 categories + automatic CSAM escalation - Content filter calibrated for PNP: doesn’t block community vocabulary, does block real harm vectors (slamming, bug chasing, non-consent, weapons, drug sales) - Multi-vector bans: when someone is removed, all their known identifiers are blocked - Public appeal system for bans

Active wellness - Wellness Mode with 24h cooling-off (intentional friction) - Private use tracker (slam/smoke) - Self-Care Center without ads or algorithm - Wellness Hangouts

How we measure whether we’re keeping our word

We don’t want you to take our word for it. These are the numbers we publish:

  • % of earnings going to creators (published in real time at pnptv.app/docs)
  • Sober Body days accumulated by the community (aggregated, anonymous)
  • % of creators using the Self-Care Center at least once/month
  • Support tickets resolved in under 48h
  • Number of creators with active access to wellness fund services

If the numbers aren’t being published, ask. If the answers aren’t satisfactory, write to us at support@pnptv.app.

A final word

We know “platform that cares for its creators” sounds like marketing. We’ve seen it too. The difference is that this was written by people who’ve been on the other side — who know the exhaustion, the family stigma, the night when use stopped being recreational, the loneliness of having no one to call when something goes wrong.

We’re not perfect. There are gaps in this document that we’re being honest enough to name. But the direction is clear and we’re not changing it: an adult platform that treats its creators like adults — with respect, with resources, and with the conviction that their wellbeing isn’t an operating cost but the reason we exist.
