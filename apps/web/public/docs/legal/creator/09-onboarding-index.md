## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the PNPtv! Creator Partner Program. You are receiving this package because your application has been approved. We are a queer PNP adult creator platform, built by and for a community that has been marginalized, moralized at, and abandoned by mainstream services. Our house standard is **welfare-first and duty-of-care**: our priority is that you build a sustainable, dignified creative career here — not that we squeeze you for short-term revenue. That priority is codified across every document in this package.

You are joining a small, deliberately-selected group of creators. The Partner Program is not the general creator tier — it is the tier where you get a dedicated ops contact, a direct Slack channel, priority weighting on feature requests, DMCA support, welfare resources, and a real 70/30 economic deal. In return, you commit to a professional standard: on-time on scheduled slots, prompt on Slack, respectful in the community, no off-platform diversion, no drama. The next few documents describe both sides of that deal in detail.

*— Santino, on behalf of the PNPtv! team*

## 3. Recommended Reading Order

**08 → 01 → 02 → 03 → 05 → 04 → 07 → 06**

Rationale for the order:

- **08** first — the plain-English overview so nothing in the contracts is a surprise.
- **01 → 02 → 03** — the three signable contracts, in the order they reference each other (NDA is Document 1 of the contract set; the Partner Agreement incorporates the NDA and the Content License by reference).
- **05** before **04** — the Communications Policy defines the channels the Code of Conduct enforces on. Easier to understand the enforcement rules if you already know which channels are which.
- **04** — the full behavior standard and the enforcement ladder that all four policies share.
- **07** — the anti-poaching detail. Reading it after the Code of Conduct makes the "faster ladder" language make sense.
- **06** — last, because the Telegram group is opt-in and its guidelines are subordinate to the policies you've already read.

## 4. Onboarding Steps Checklist

Numbered, in order. Steps 1–4 are prerequisites for earning; steps 5–10 get you operational and paid.

1. **Read the Rights & Responsibilities Charter** (Doc 08). ~10 min. This is the shape of the deal.
2. **Complete 2257 identity verification** via the platform's AI-verified ID upload flow. No monetization is released until 2257 is complete and in good standing (Doc 02 § 7.4).
3. **Read and sign the three contracts** in order:
   - Doc 01 — NDA
   - Doc 02 — Partner Program Agreement
   - Doc 03 — Content License Addendum
   Delivered for signature via **[SIGNING PLATFORM TBD — flag for Santino to pick: DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, or SignNow]**.
4. **Read and acknowledge the four policies:**
   - Doc 04 — Code of Conduct
   - Doc 05 — Communications Policy
   - Doc 06 — Telegram Group Guidelines (only if you join the group)
   - Doc 07 — Anti-Poaching & Non-Circumvention Policy
   Acknowledgment is captured in the same signing flow (checkbox per policy).
5. **Set up your creator profile** in the PNPtv! webapp — bio, avatar, rates, availability, working-hours preference (this is what your Slack SLA is measured against).
6. **Your Slack `#ext-[handle]` channel gets provisioned** by ops on your behalf. You receive an invite email. **Introduce yourself there** — a couple of sentences about you and your working hours.
7. **Book your Creator Onboarding Call** via Cal.com — 30 minutes with Santino or Miguel. Walk-through of tools, expectations, Q&A.
8. **Attend the onboarding call.** Leave with your first-week plan (first stream date, first booking window, first VOD upload target).
9. **Go operational:**
   - Set up your first scheduled stream (added to the discovery grid), and/or
   - Open your booking calendar for private calls, and/or
   - Upload your first PNP Channels video.
10. **Get paid.** First payout batch after your accrued balance hits **USD $100** at the weekly batch cutoff. Weekly batches run on the day PNPtv! designates (posted on the platform).
