# PNPtv! Creator Partner Program — Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**Document 1 of 3 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.**

---

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

Este Acuerdo de Confidencialidad Mutua (NDA) protege la información confidencial que las partes intercambien en el marco del Programa de Socios Creadores de PNPtv!. Aunque las obligaciones son mutuas, la protección se orienta principalmente a la información sensible de PNPtv! (código fuente, hojas de ruta, datos de ingresos, identidad y ganancias de otros creadores, PII de miembros, contenido interno de Slack/Telegram, datos del CRM Zoho, programas de pruebas internas, resultados de la IA "Cristina", estados financieros y planes de negocio) dado que el Creador tendrá exposición a la operación interna. El Acuerdo permanece vigente durante toda la participación en el Programa y **sobrevive por 2 (dos) años** tras su terminación. Se rige por la ley colombiana e incluye referencia expresa a la **Ley 1581 de 2012** (Habeas Data) por el acceso incidental que el Creador pueda tener a datos personales de miembros. Las controversias se resolverán mediante **arbitraje en el Centro de Arbitraje y Conciliación de la Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá D.C.**

## 2. Purpose

**2.1.** The Parties intend to explore, negotiate, and perform activities under the Creator Partner Program, which includes (but is not limited to) content production, live streaming, private video calls, hangouts, VOD publication on PNP Channels, tipping, subscriptions, and cross-promotion (the **"Purpose"**).

**2.2.** In pursuit of the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other certain non-public information. This NDA governs the treatment of such information.

---

## 3. Definition of Confidential Information

**3.1.** **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the **"Disclosing Party"**) to the other (the **"Receiving Party"**), whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by observation, and whether or not marked as confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

**3.2.** Without limiting the generality of Section 3.1, and given the asymmetric access the Creator will have to internal PNPtv! operations, Confidential Information **explicitly includes** the following (non-exhaustive list):

**(a) Technical & Product Information.**
- Platform source code, database schemas, architectural diagrams, deployment topology, security posture, and any credentials or access tokens.
- Unreleased features, product roadmaps, and internal experiments — including but not limited to the Ru$h token currency roadmap, Phase 2 initiatives, and the "Chase the Clouds" tester program.
- Internal AI systems, prompts, outputs, and moderation signals — including the Cristina AI assistant and Mono notification system.

**(b) Financial & Commercial Information.**
- Revenue data, splits, gross and net figures, aggregate creator earnings, and PNPtv!'s internal financial statements, forecasts, and business plans.
- Individual earnings, tips received, tokens spent/earned, subscription counts, and any other financial metric of any **other creator or performer** on the platform.
- Payment processor arrangements, chargeback data, and fee structures.

**(c) People & Community Information.**
- The **identities, handles, real names, contact details, geolocation, viewing habits, or Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** of any platform member, subscriber, or user.
- The identities, handles, real names, contact details, or PII of any **other creator or performer** on the platform, except to the extent already publicly displayed on the creator's own profile.
- Testing-program tester identities and any content posted in the `#testing-chase-the-clouds` Slack channel or equivalent.

**(d) Internal Communications & Records.**
- Contents of internal Slack channels (including but not limited to the Creator's own `#ext-[handle]` channel to the extent the content reflects internal PNPtv! discussion), internal Telegram channels, email threads, meeting notes, and any recordings thereof.
- Zoho CRM records — including Leads, Contacts, notes, and pipeline data.
- Support tickets, moderation actions, 2257 record submissions, and their metadata.
- Draft and unreleased marketing, PR, and legal materials.

**(e) Business Strategy Information.**
- Partnerships (in negotiation or executed), vendor relationships, hiring plans, and internal org changes.
- Growth strategies, pricing experiments, and A/B test data.

**3.3.** Confidential Information also includes any analysis, summary, or derivative work created by the Receiving Party that is based on or incorporates Confidential Information.

---

## 4. Exclusions

**4.1.** Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

- **(a)** was in the public domain at the time of disclosure, or entered the public domain thereafter through no breach of this NDA by the Receiving Party;
- **(b)** was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, free of any confidentiality obligation;
- **(c)** was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
- **(d)** was rightfully received from a third party who had the right to disclose it without confidentiality restriction.

---

## 5. Obligations of the Receiving Party

**5.1.** The Receiving Party shall:

- **(a)** hold Confidential Information in strict confidence and use at least the same degree of care as it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care;
- **(b)** use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
- **(c)** disclose Confidential Information only to those of its professional advisors, contractors, or (if applicable) employees who have a legitimate need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this NDA;
- **(d)** not reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to derive source code from any Confidential Information disclosed in compiled or binary form;
- **(e)** not use Confidential Information to compete with the Disclosing Party, poach its members or creators, or otherwise unfairly benefit at its expense; and
- **(f)** promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

**5.2.** The Creator specifically agrees not to disclose, publish, or discuss any Confidential Information on any social media platform, in any interview, in any group chat outside of PNPtv!-approved channels, or with any third party (including romantic partners, friends, family, agents, or managers) without PNPtv!'s prior written consent — except to professional advisors bound by professional secrecy (e.g., attorneys, tax advisors, medical professionals).

---

## 6. Permitted Disclosures

**6.1.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if and to the extent required by:

- **(a)** applicable law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other legal or governmental process; or
- **(b)** a bona fide investigation by a regulatory authority with jurisdiction (in Colombia, the United States, or elsewhere).

**6.2.** In each case, the Receiving Party shall (unless legally prohibited): **(i)** give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice sufficient to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy; **(ii)** cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain such remedy; and **(iii)** disclose only the minimum amount of Confidential Information legally required.

---

## 7. Data Protection — Ley 1581 de 2012 (Habeas Data)

**7.1.** The Parties acknowledge that certain Confidential Information constitutes **personal data of members** under Colombian **Ley 1581 de 2012** and its regulatory decrees ("**Habeas Data**").

**7.2.** With respect to any personal data of members, subscribers, or other creators that the Creator may access or receive:

- **(a)** the Creator shall process such data solely for the Purpose and consistent with PNPtv!'s Privacy Policy and the instructions of PNPtv! (which is the "Responsable del Tratamiento" of member data);
- **(b)** the Creator shall not disclose, transfer, sell, or otherwise share such data with any third party;
- **(c)** the Creator shall implement reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect such data against loss, unauthorized access, or misuse;
- **(d)** the Creator shall promptly notify PNPtv! (within twenty-four (24) hours) of any suspected or actual data-security incident affecting such data; and
- **(e)** upon termination of the Purpose (or on written request), the Creator shall return or securely destroy such data and, on request, certify destruction in writing.

**7.3.** For the avoidance of doubt, the Creator acts as an **Encargado del Tratamiento** with respect to any personal data of members disclosed by PNPtv!, and PNPtv! remains the Responsable del Tratamiento.

## 8. Ownership; No License

**8.1.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any license, express or implied, under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

## 9. Term and Survival

**9.1.** **Term.** This NDA takes effect on the date of signature by the last Party to sign (the **"Effective Date"**) and continues in force for the entire period during which the Creator participates in the Partner Program.

**9.2.** **Survival.** The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality under this NDA shall survive termination of the Partner Program for a period of **two (2) years** from the date the Creator ceases participation.

**9.3.** **Trade Secrets.** Notwithstanding Section 9.2, information that qualifies as a **trade secret** under applicable law shall remain protected as Confidential Information for as long as it retains trade-secret status.

---

## 10. Return or Destruction of Materials

**10.1.** On termination of the Partner Program or upon written request by the Disclosing Party at any time, the Receiving Party shall, within **fifteen (15) business days**:

- **(a)** return all tangible materials embodying Confidential Information; and
- **(b)** permanently delete all electronic copies of Confidential Information from all devices, cloud storage, email accounts, and backups reasonably within its control (excluding routine, non-targeted backup archives that will be overwritten in the ordinary course).

**10.2.** On written request, the Receiving Party shall certify in writing that the obligations of Section 10.1 have been satisfied.

---

## 11. Remedies

**11.1.** **Injunctive Relief.** The Parties acknowledge that a breach of this NDA may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek **injunctive relief and specific performance** ("medidas cautelares") in any court of competent jurisdiction (or from the arbitral tribunal) in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

**11.2.** **Liquidated Damages.** The Parties agree that, in the event of a material breach of this NDA by the Creator (in particular Sections 5.1 or 7.2), PNPtv! shall be entitled to liquidated damages ("cláusula penal") in the amount specified in the Partner Agreement (Document 2), Section on Liquidated Damages, without prejudice to the recovery of any additional damages actually suffered.

**11.3.** **Attorneys' Fees.** The prevailing Party in any action to enforce this NDA shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

## 13. Miscellaneous

**13.1.** **Entire Agreement.** This NDA, together with Documents 2 and 3 of the Creator Partner Program legal package, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior discussions.

**13.2.** **Amendment.** This NDA may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.

**13.3.** **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the other Party's prior written consent, except that PNPtv! may assign to an affiliate or in connection with a corporate reorganization, merger, or sale of substantially all assets.

**13.4.** **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**13.5.** **No Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision shall not be a waiver of the right to enforce it later.

**13.6.** **Independent Contractors.** Nothing in this NDA creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the Parties.

**13.7.** **Notices.** Notices shall be sent (a) to PNPtv! via the Creator's `#ext-[handle]` Slack channel with copy to `legal@pnptv.app`, and (b) to the Creator via the same Slack channel and the email address on file with the platform. Notices are effective on delivery.

## 14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**14.1.** **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of Colombia**, including without limitation the Código Civil, Código de Comercio, and Ley 1581 de 2012.

**14.2.** **US Federal Compliance.** Notwithstanding the choice of Colombian law, the Parties acknowledge that PNPtv! serves a substantial US audience and is therefore also subject to **18 U.S.C. § 2257** (record-keeping requirements for adult content) as a separate and independent obligation. Nothing in this NDA relieves either Party of applicable US federal compliance obligations.

**14.3.** **Arbitration.** Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this NDA, or the breach, termination, enforcement, interpretation, or validity thereof, shall be finally resolved by **arbitration administered by the Centro de Arbitraje y Conciliación de la Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá D.C.**, in accordance with its rules in effect at the time of the request for arbitration.

**14.4.** **Tribunal.** The arbitration shall be conducted by **one (1) arbitrator**, in **Spanish** (unless the Parties agree in writing to English), and the seat of arbitration shall be **Bogotá D.C., Colombia**. The award shall be final and binding, and judgment upon the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**14.5.** **Interim Relief.** Notwithstanding Section 14.3, either Party may seek interim, provisional, or conservatory relief ("medidas cautelares") from any court of competent jurisdiction pending the arbitral tribunal's determination of the merits.
