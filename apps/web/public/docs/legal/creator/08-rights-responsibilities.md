# PNPtv! Creator Rights & Responsibilities Charter

**Document 8 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.** This is a plain-English charter, not a contract. It summarizes — in human terms — what PNPtv! commits to you and what you commit to PNPtv!. The binding legal terms live in Documents 1 (NDA), 2 (Partner Agreement), and 3 (Content License). If you ever notice a conflict between this Charter and those documents, the signed documents control — but tell us, because the Charter should not be misleading.

---

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

### Tus Derechos (en 3 frases)

PNPtv! te garantiza un **reparto de ingresos 70/30** neto de comisiones del procesador, con lotes de pago semanales desde USD $100 y retiros a demanda desde USD $50 en USDT/USDC. Tienes un **canal Slack `#ext-[handle]`** con respuesta humana en máximo **12 horas hábiles** (según tu horario declarado), soporte legal 2257, apoyo antipiratería DMCA, y acceso al **Programa de Cuidado del Creador** con enfoque de reducción de daños. La aplicación de sanciones sigue la escalera del Código de Conducta (coaching → advertencia → suspensión → terminación) con **derecho de apelación de 14 días**.

### Tus Responsabilidades (en 3 frases)

Debes **completar la verificación 2257** antes de ganar, mantener estándares de contenido (18+, consentimiento, sin menores, sin no-consentimiento, sin bestialidad, sin venta de sustancias) y cumplir con avisos de puntualidad (2 horas para cancelar llamadas privadas, 30 minutos para streams programados). **Toda comunicación de trabajo va por Slack `#ext-[handle]` o la plataforma PNPtv!** — nunca WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram personal, ni SMS — y **nunca redirigir miembros fuera de plataforma** (política de no-elusión Documento 7). Eres **contratista independiente** responsable de tus impuestos DIAN / IRS y del cumplimiento del NDA (confidencialidad de 2 años post-terminación) y la no-solicitación (cola de 12 meses post-terminación).

## SECTION A — What PNPtv! Commits to YOU (Your Rights)

### A.1. Fair revenue share — 70/30, transparent, net of processor fees

- **The split:** You receive **70% of Net Revenue**; PNPtv! retains 30%. Payment-processor fees (NowPayments etc.) are deducted from gross **before** the 70/30 split, so both sides share proportionally in processor cost. See **Partner Agreement (Doc 2) § 5.1**.
- **Transparent ledger:** Your Creator Dashboard shows views, plays, tips received, subscription counts, earnings, and clip/promo usage — see **Content License (Doc 3) § 10**.
- **Currency rate is fixed:** Base rate is **1 USD = 6 Ru$h**. When your Ru$h earnings settle to USD, that base rate applies unless a superseding rate is posted at the time of the transaction. See **Doc 2 § 5.2**.

### A.2. Timely payouts

- **Weekly batch payouts:** Runs on the day PNPtv! designates. Minimum balance to be included: **USD $100**. Balances below roll forward. See **Doc 2 § 6.1**.
- **On-demand cashouts:** Minimum **USD $50** per request. Target processing: 5 business days (not guaranteed given crypto settlement + compliance-review windows). See **Doc 2 § 6.2**.
- **Preferred payout rails:** USDT or USDC (TRC-20 or ERC-20). Bank transfer available where feasible in your jurisdiction. See **Doc 2 § 6.3**.
- **Gifted tokens do NOT accrue earnings.** Only tokens purchased by members with real money generate your earnings. This is enforced in the token ledger and is not subject to case-by-case override. See **Doc 2 § 5.3**.
- **No hidden payout fees.** PNPtv! does not charge above actual network/processor cost, and those costs are disclosed on your dashboard. See **Doc 2 § 6.5**.

### A.3. Working tools that actually work

- **Full feature set:** Live streams, private video calls, group hangouts, VOD on PNP Channels, DMs, the Main Stage broadcast surface, tipping, subscriptions, Ru$h currency, discovery placement.
- **Ops team owns uptime.** Bugs and outages surface to `#creator-support` (see Communications Policy Doc 5 § 2.2) and get worked. Automated notifications (booking-starting-soon, payout receipts, ID reminders) come via `@PNPLatinoTV_bot` — see **Doc 5 § 2.6**.

### A.4. Marketing and promotion

- **Discovery placement.** You are listed in platform discovery surfaces subject to your tier and standing. PRIME creators get a discovery boost across feed, featured, nearby, search, and mentions. See **Doc 2 § 4.1(c)**.
- **Promotional clip creation.** PNPtv! generates promo excerpts (up to 60 seconds) from your Content for use in "coming up on PNPtv!" carousels, best-of reels, in-app banners, and outbound social amplification. See **Content License (Doc 3) § 3.1(g)**.
- **Attribution is standard.** Your handle is displayed on featured Content in a manner visible to viewers — see **Doc 3 § 4.1**.
- **Approved external social channels:** X (formerly Twitter), the PNPtv! newsletter, and any others expressly designated in writing. **PNPtv! does not operate on Bluesky or Discord** and your Content is never pushed there. See **Doc 3 § 3.2**.

### A.5. A dedicated Slack channel and real human response

- **Your `#ext-[handle]` channel** is your direct line to Santino, Carlos, Miguel, and Cristina AI (first-line acknowledgment 24/7). See **Doc 5 § 2.1**.
- **SLA:** Business-day acknowledgment within **12 hours** during your stated working hours (both directions — you to us, us to you). Not resolution — acknowledgment. See **Doc 5 § 2.1**.
- **You're never talking to a bot alone.** Cristina AI provides fast first-line acknowledgment, but a human always closes the loop.

### A.6. Welfare and Duty of Care

- **Access to the Creator Care Program** — mental-health resources, crisis-referral path, peer support via the Partner Program Telegram group, harm-reduction guidance, referral to community organizations when needed. See `/docs/creator-care-program-en.html`.
- **Warm handoff for you.** If you need reduced availability for a period, note it in Slack `#ext-[handle]` and ops will pause your discovery placements and communicate to booked members on your behalf. See **Doc 5 § 6.3**.
- **PNPtv! is welfare-first.** We do not moralize at the PNP community. We are not a rehabilitation program. We are a safe haven staffed by people who understand. See **Code of Conduct (Doc 4) § 1.2**.

### A.7. Legal and 2257 support

- **Custodian of Records** handles 2257 record-keeping under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75. Custodian identity and business address are posted on the platform's 2257 Compliance Notice. See **Doc 2 § 7.3**.
- **AI-verified 2257 upload flow** (currently xAI grok-4.3 vision + human admin review) makes verification fast. See **Doc 2 § 7.2**.
- **DMCA takedown support at no additional charge** — PNPtv! issues 17 U.S.C. § 512 notices to hosting providers, search engines, and social platforms when your Content is pirated. Colombian equivalents under Ley 23 de 1982 and Ley 1915 de 2018 also invoked where applicable. See **Doc 3 § 7.1**.
- **Evidentiary support** for counter-notice or dispute — upload logs, hash records, timestamps. See **Doc 3 § 7.1(c)**.

### A.8. Privacy and safety

- **Control over your public profile.** You decide what personal data is visible.
- **Right to be forgotten** with reasonable business-necessity exceptions under Colombian **Ley 1581 de 2012 (Habeas Data)** — see **NDA (Doc 1) § 7**.
- **Right to block members.** Enforced in-platform.
- **Safety escalation for doxxing or harassment.** SMS/phone to Santino reserved for this scenario. `@cristina help` in any Slack channel pages the on-call ops person. See **Doc 5 § 6.2**.

### A.9. Fair enforcement — the ladder applies to us too

- **Consistent enforcement ladder** across all four policy documents: **coaching (Step 1) → written warning (Step 2) → suspension with earnings frozen not confiscated (Step 3) → termination for cause (Step 4)**. See **Code of Conduct (Doc 4) § 5**.
- **Due process on the way up.** You get written notice, the opportunity to respond, and ops review before any permanent action.
- **Right to appeal within 14 days.** Any decision at Step 2 or above is appealable once to Santino (if not already the decision-maker). External-adviser channel `oversight@pnptv.app` available if your concern involves staff — see **Doc 4 § 6.3**.
- **Warnings reset after 12 months** with no repeat behavior (6 months for Telegram-group-specific warnings per Doc 6 § 6.5). See **Doc 4 § 5.6**.
- **Zero-tolerance items** (Doc 4 § 2) begin higher on the ladder — but the same due-process protections apply.

### A.10. Voice in the platform

- **Feature requests and product feedback** in `#creator-support` (Doc 5 § 2.2). Ops sees it and tracks it.
- **Priority weighting** for Partner Program creators on feature requests.
- **Quarterly Creator Forum call** — once the Partner Program hits 20+ creators, we schedule a quarterly all-hands.

### A.11. Non-exclusivity — you are not our property

- **PNPtv! is non-exclusive.** You can operate on OnlyFans, Fansly, JustForFans, Fanvue, any other adult platform, or independently, in parallel. No disclosure required. See **Anti-Poaching Policy (Doc 7) § 3.1**.
- **Cross-promotion encouraged.** Post about your PNPtv! streams on your other socials; link to your PNPtv! profile from anywhere. See **Doc 7 § 3.2**.
- **PNPtv! is your co-worker, not your owner.**

### A.12. Post-termination — a clean exit

- **90-day content-export window** — data-export tool to download your own uploaded Content (source files where feasible; otherwise best-available master). See **Doc 2 § 13.6(a)** and **Doc 3 § 6.5**.
- **Final payout within 30 days** of the effective termination date, subject to any offsets. See **Doc 2 § 13.6(b)**.
- **Already-published promotional clips may remain live for 90 days**, after which they are deprecated. See **Doc 3 § 6.4**.
- **Full-length Content on public surfaces:** PNPtv! ceases publishing within **30 days** of your written termination request. See **Doc 3 § 6.3(a)**.

### A.13. A community without drama

- **The Code of Conduct binds every creator.** Trash-talking colleagues, drama, poaching regulars, coordinated pile-ons — all triggerable behaviors. See **Doc 4 § 3** and **Doc 4 § 2**.
- **Your peer experience is protected too.** Moderated Partner Program Telegram group. See **Doc 6**.

## SECTION B — What YOU Commit to PNPtv! (Your Responsibilities)

### B.1. 2257 compliance — non-negotiable

- **Valid government ID** via the AI-verified upload flow before you earn a cent. See **Doc 2 § 7.1**.
- **Keep it current** — re-verify if it expires or if you feature new performers.
- **No monetization without verification.** If 2257 lapses, PNPtv! suspends monetization until re-verification. See **Doc 2 § 7.4**.

### B.2. Content standards

- **All content 18+**, consent-first, no minors, no non-consensual conduct, no bestiality, no drug-sale transactions on the platform. See **Doc 2 § 8.2(b)**.
- **Harm-reduction messaging** where PNP substances come up — never encourage unsafe combos, never counsel a member in acute distress (refer instead). See **Doc 4 § 4.3**.
- **Chain of rights** — written consent from every other person appearing in your Content. See **Doc 3 § 8.2**.
- **No pirated content from other creators.** Uploading another creator's content without written permission is grounds for termination for cause. See **Doc 3 § 7.3**.

### B.3. Punctuality on scheduled work

- **Booked private calls:** 2-hour cancellation notice. See **Doc 4 § 3.8**.
- **Scheduled streams (in the discovery grid):** 30-minute cancellation notice.
- **No-shows have consequences.** Repeated no-shows damage member trust and platform reputation and are grounds for escalation. See **Doc 4 § 3.8**.

### B.4. Response SLA on Slack `#ext-[handle]`

- **Acknowledge within 12 business hours** during your stated working hours. See **Doc 5 § 2.1**.
- **Chronic silence** on your ops channel signals bad-faith participation and triggers the ladder. See **Doc 4 § 3.9**.

### B.5. Communications discipline

- **Slack + PNPtv! platform only** for platform business. See **Doc 5 § 2** and **Doc 5 § 3**.
- **Prohibited channels for platform business:** WhatsApp, Instagram DM, personal Telegram, SMS/iMessage, personal email, Signal, Discord, Bluesky, Snapchat, and any other channel not listed in **Doc 5 § 2**. See **Doc 5 § 3**.
- **Standard redirect template** when members reach you off-platform — Doc 5 § 4.1 (English) and § 4.2 (Spanish). Do not begin the conversation off-platform "just this once."

### B.6. No drama, no gossip

- **No public airing of grievances** about other creators, members, or the platform. Concerns go to Slack `#ext-[handle]` (private) or `#creator-support` (peer + ops). See **Doc 4 § 3.1**.
- **No trash-talking competitor creators.** See **Doc 4 § 3.2**.
- **No complaining about members publicly.** Bad-actor members are reported via the in-platform `/report` flow. See **Doc 4 § 3.4**.
- **Escalate internally before speaking publicly** about platform issues. See **Doc 4 § 3.5**.

### B.7. No poaching, no circumvention

- **Members flow through the platform.** Directing a PNPtv! member to pay via OnlyFans, Fansly, PayPal, Zelle, Cash App, direct crypto, bank transfer, or any other channel where PNPtv! is not the counterparty is **direct diversion of revenue** — not a "fee-avoidance courtesy." See **Anti-Poaching Policy (Doc 7) § 2.1**.
- **No contact-swap for paid services.** See **Doc 7 § 2.3**.
- **No "avoid the platform fee" framing.** See **Doc 7 § 2.4**.
- **No recruiting other PNPtv! creators to a competing platform** during participation + 12 months post-term. See **Doc 7 § 2.5**.
- **No recruiting PNPtv! staff** during participation + 12 months post-term. See **Doc 7 § 2.6**.
- **Detection is real** — member surveys, chargeback patterns, direct reports, on-platform DM moderation, industry intelligence, payment reconciliation gaps. See **Doc 7 § 4**.
- **Enforcement escalates fast** on Doc 7 — first offense includes retroactive revenue-share adjustment for the detected leakage. See **Doc 7 § 5**.

### B.8. Confidentiality — the NDA is real

- **Confidential Information includes:** platform source code and roadmap, revenue data and financials, **other creators' identities and earnings**, member PII, internal Slack, Zoho CRM records, Cristina AI outputs, testing-program content, unreleased marketing. See **NDA (Doc 1) § 3**.
- **Survives 2 years post-termination.** Trade secrets survive as long as they remain trade secrets. See **Doc 1 § 9.2** and **§ 9.3**.
- **No screenshotting internal Slack or the Partner Program Telegram group to external audiences.** See **Doc 4 § 3.10** and **Doc 6 § 3.7 – 3.8**.
- **You may discuss your own experience in general terms** — you may not disclose other creators' earnings, aggregate platform data, or internal Slack content.

### B.9. Content license grant

- **Non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license** to PNPtv! to reproduce, transmit, distribute, display, transcode, thumbnail, and clip your Content for delivery on platform surfaces. See **Content License (Doc 3) § 3**.
- **Promotional derivative clips of up to 60 seconds** may be used across approved surfaces. See **Doc 3 § 3.1(g)**.
- **You retain ownership** of the underlying Content — see **Doc 3 § 2**.
- **Post-term promotional window:** existing 60-second promo clips may remain in use for **12 months** post-term; no new promotional derivatives created after termination. See **Doc 3 § 6.2**.

### B.10. Tax responsibility

- **You are an independent contractor** under a **contrato de prestación de servicios** — no employment relationship. See **Doc 2 § 3**.
- **Your own DIAN / IRS / home-jurisdiction taxes.** RUT registration where applicable, invoicing per DIAN standards, income tax filings, IVA where applicable. See **Doc 2 § 17**.
- **PNPtv! may withhold** *retención en la fuente* where required by Colombian tax law and remit to DIAN with certificate.
- **Non-Colombian residents:** you're responsible for local tax compliance; PNPtv! may request tax-residency certifications. See **Doc 2 § 17.3**.

### B.11. Truthful representation

- **Persona is welcome** — stage names, characters, fictional backstories.
- **Identity fraud is not** — impersonating a real person to deceive members for money, or catfishing in a way that would ground a member's financial reliance on a materially false identity. See **Doc 4 § 2.9**.
- **Never misrepresent age** — yours or any performer's. See **Doc 2 § 7.1** and **Doc 4 § 2.11**.

### B.12. Report harm — see something, say something

- **Bad-actor members** → in-platform `/report` flow, or Slack `#creator-support` if you need creator-side coordination. See **Doc 4 § 6.1**.
- **Another creator behaving badly** → Slack DM to Santino or Miguel, or `report@pnptv.app`, or the anonymous form (Doc 4 § 6.2).
- **A staff member behaving badly** → `oversight@pnptv.app` (external adviser, not read by the person being reported) or the anonymous form. See **Doc 4 § 6.3**.
- **Members in crisis** → warm handoff to the Creator Care Program crisis-resource path per **Doc 4 § 4.6**.
- **No retaliation** against good-faith reporters — retaliation is a **Section 2.12 zero-tolerance violation**. See **Doc 4 § 6.4**.

### B.13. Represent PNPtv! well in public

- **Promote the platform freely** — encouraged. Brand use for this purpose is granted under **Doc 2 § 4.1(d)** and **Doc 5 § 7.1**.
- **Do not speak on behalf of PNPtv!** to press, regulators, law enforcement, or industry organizations without prior written approval from Santino. See **Doc 5 § 7.2**.
- **Refer journalists** to `press@pnptv.app`. Refer law-enforcement inquiries to `legal@pnptv.app` and tell ops in Slack immediately.
- **Your own opinions remain yours** — including opinions critical of PNPtv! — subject to internal-escalation-first (Doc 4 § 3.5) and confidentiality (Doc 1).

## 2. The Reciprocity

**2.1.** PNPtv! is investing in you: infrastructure, marketing, member acquisition, 2257 compliance, payment processing, DMCA defense, safety escalation, welfare support, legal cover, discovery placement, brand.

**2.2.** You are investing in PNPtv!: your work, your presence, your community reputation, your professionalism, your good faith.

**2.3.** The goal is a **sustainable, dignified, well-compensated creative career** for people in a community that mainstream platforms have historically marginalized, criminalized, moralized at, and abandoned. PNPtv! exists so that the PNP creator community has a serious, professionally-run place to work — with people who understand.

**2.4.** The commitments in Section A make that possible from our side. The commitments in Section B make it possible from yours. Neither side works without the other.
