# PNPtv! Creator Communications Policy

**Policy Document 5 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.** This is a policy, not a per-signature contract. Creators agree to it by joining the Partner Program; the Partner Agreement (Document 2) incorporates it by reference.

---

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

Esta Política de Comunicaciones define los **canales aprobados** para todo asunto relacionado con PNPtv! y prohíbe los canales fuera-de-plataforma para el trabajo del Programa de Socios. Existe para tres razones concretas: (1) **protección legal** — cuando la comunicación laboral vive en WhatsApp o Instagram, PNPtv! no puede responder por lo que sucede allí; (2) **protección del Creador** — el acoso, la coerción y las estafas prosperan en canales sin moderación; (3) **protección de ingresos** — cada conversación de trabajo fuera-de-plataforma es una fuga potencial hacia otro servicio.

**Canales aprobados**: Slack `#ext-[handle]` (privado, ops y pagos), Slack `#creator-support` (comunidad de creadores + ops), Slack `#creator-announcements` (solo lectura), mensajería in-app de PNPtv! (con miembros), y el grupo de Telegram del Programa de Socios (informal — ver Documento 6). **Canales prohibidos** para asuntos de la plataforma: WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Telegram personal, SMS/iMessage, correo personal, Signal, Discord, Bluesky, Snap. Cuando un miembro te escriba fuera de plataforma, respondes con la plantilla y lo rediriges. Escalada por incumplimiento repetido según la **escalera de sanciones del Código de Conducta** (Documento 4).

## 1. Purpose

**1.1.** This Policy defines the **only channels** through which Partner Program business is conducted, and prohibits off-channel communication for platform-related work.

**1.2.** It exists for three concrete reasons:

- **(a) Legal protection.** When work communication lives on WhatsApp or Instagram, PNPtv! cannot supervise, log, moderate, subpoena, or defend what happens there. That exposes creators, members, and the platform.
- **(b) Creator protection.** Harassment, coercion, stalking, and scams thrive in unmoderated one-on-one channels. Keeping work in monitored channels is a safety feature, not surveillance.
- **(c) Revenue protection.** Every work conversation that leaves the platform is a potential leak to a competing service. See Document 7 (Anti-Poaching & Non-Circumvention Policy).

**1.3.** This Policy is a standard of professional practice, not employment supervision. Creators remain independent contractors and are free to use any personal channel for personal communication that is unrelated to PNPtv! work.

## 2. Approved Channels — Where Platform Business Happens

These are the **only** channels for Partner Program work.

### 2.1. Slack `#ext-[handle]` — Your primary ops channel.

- **Format:** Shared private Slack channel between the individual Creator and the PNPtv! ops team. One per Creator. Named `#ext-[your-handle]`.
- **Used for:** payouts, scheduling, incident reports, feature requests, escalations, tax/1099-equivalent questions, 2257 record updates, promo slot requests, private complaints about specific members, anything that names a specific person or a specific dollar amount.
- **Response SLA — creator to ops:** business-day acknowledgment within **12 hours** during the creator's stated working hours (working-hours preference is set once in your Creator profile and can be updated in Slack).
- **Response SLA — ops to creator:** business-day acknowledgment within **12 hours** (Colombia local business days). Cristina AI Slackbot provides first-line acknowledgment 24/7.
- **Not for:** peer-to-peer creator chat (that's the Telegram group per Document 6) or member-facing conversations (that's in-platform DM).

### 2.2. Slack `#creator-support` — Group ops + peer channel.

- **Format:** Shared Slack channel including all Partner Program creators and the PNPtv! ops team.
- **Used for:** questions with likely shared interest ("has anyone had this bug on iPad?"), bug reports, feature requests, professional feedback on platform decisions, coordination for collabs, general "does anyone know how to..." questions.
- **Not for:** payout disputes (private to `#ext-[handle]`), complaints naming specific members (private to `#ext-[handle]`), or drama about other creators (per Document 4 § 3.1, this belongs nowhere — but especially not here).

### 2.3. Slack `#creator-announcements` — Broadcast-only, read-only for creators.

- **Format:** One-way broadcast from PNPtv! (Santino, Miguel, or ops designee).
- **Used for:** product updates, policy changes, incident notifications, promotional pushes, invitation to opt-in campaigns, notice of amendments to any policy in this legal package.
- Threaded replies enabled for clarifying questions; substantive discussion moves to `#creator-support`.

### 2.4. PNPtv! platform in-app messaging.

- **Used for:** all member-facing communication. Direct messages with members, live-stream chat, hangout chat, private-call chat, tip acknowledgments, unlockable-message threads.
- **All member messages happen here.** Not elsewhere. See Section 4 for the redirect template when members approach off-platform.

### 2.5. Partner Program Telegram group(s).

- **Format:** Informal peer chat group for Partner Program creators, moderated per **Document 6 (Telegram Group Guidelines)**.
- **Used for:** peer greetings, birthday shout-outs, wins, casual creator-to-creator questions, mutual support, occasional platform news previews.
- **Not for:** ops requests, financial matters, member complaints, or anything that belongs in Slack. Explicitly not a support-ticket queue.

### 2.6. Platform-generated Telegram alerts from `@PNPLatinoTV_bot`.

- **Direction:** platform → creator only.
- **Used for:** booked-call-starting-soon alerts, payout receipts, ID-verification reminders, other automated notifications.
- Do not reply to `@PNPLatinoTV_bot` expecting a human — it does not carry two-way messaging. Escalate through Slack.

## 3. Prohibited Channels for Platform Business

The following channels are **prohibited** for any Partner Program work — with members, other creators, or PNPtv! staff. This is an exhaustive list; if a channel is not in Section 2, treat it as prohibited.

- **(a)** WhatsApp (personal or business account);
- **(b)** Instagram DM;
- **(c)** Personal Telegram (the creator's individual Telegram account, outside the Partner Program group);
- **(d)** SMS / iMessage;
- **(e)** Personal email (the creator's or the member's — all email work with PNPtv! goes through the addresses PNPtv! provides);
- **(f)** Signal;
- **(g)** Discord;
- **(h)** Bluesky;
- **(i)** Snapchat / Snap;
- **(j)** Any other messenger, video-call, or forum service not listed in Section 2.

**3.1. Public social media** (X/Twitter, Instagram feed, TikTok, YouTube, etc.) is permitted for **promotion** of your PNPtv! presence and for your personal brand generally. It is **not** an approved channel for two-way work conversation with members or with PNPtv! ops.

**3.2. Rationale for the list.** These are prohibited because PNPtv! cannot moderate, log, support, or defend what happens on them. Coercion, doxxing, off-platform solicitation, scam impersonation, and payment fraud disproportionately originate on these channels.

## 4. When a Member Reaches You Off-Platform

**4.1. Response template (English).**

> "Hey! I only take work messages on PNPtv! — meet me there for [service/DM/call/stream]: [your PNPtv! profile link]. Talk soon."

**4.2. Response template (Español).**

> "Hola! Solo respondo mensajes de trabajo por PNPtv! — nos vemos allá para [servicio/DM/llamada/stream]: [enlace a tu perfil de PNPtv!]. Nos hablamos."

**4.3. What not to do.**

- Do not begin the conversation off-platform "just this once."
- Do not accept payment off-platform.
- Do not send content off-platform.
- Do not swap a personal phone number, Instagram handle, or WhatsApp for the member's business.
- Do not agree to "sneak past the platform fee" — that is a **Document 7 Anti-Poaching violation**, not a favor.

**4.4. If the member is aggressive, threatening, or won't stop.**

- Block them on the off-platform channel where they reached you.
- Report the incident to Slack `#ext-[handle]` with a screenshot (redact any of your own PII you don't want on file).
- If threats are credible (physical harm, doxxing, family targeting), see Section 6 (Emergency Contact Tree).

**4.5. Pre-existing relationships.** If the member is someone you knew personally before PNPtv! and you have a real friendship independent of paid interaction, that friendship may continue on any channel. **Do not commercialize that friendship off-platform** — see Document 7 § 3 for the safe-path rules.

## 5. Financial Discussions

**5.1. Payout disputes, tax questions, chargebacks, refund questions, tip discrepancies — Slack `#ext-[handle]` ONLY.**

- Not in the Partner Program Telegram group.
- Not on-stream.
- Not in front of members.
- Not in DMs to Santino / Miguel outside Slack.

**5.2. Payments themselves.** All payouts are processed through the platform's payout system to the payment method on file. **PNPtv! will never ask you to accept payment or send a refund via WhatsApp, Instagram, personal email, or any prohibited channel.** If someone claiming to be PNPtv! asks you to do this, it is a phishing attempt — report immediately to Slack `#ext-[handle]` or by email to `support@pnptv.app`.

**5.3. Confidentiality.** Per the NDA (Document 1), specific dollar figures, payout amounts, and platform revenue data are confidential. Discussing your own gross earnings in general terms with peers or in press is fine; disclosing another creator's earnings, or aggregate platform data, is not.

## 6. Emergency Contact Tree

For genuine emergencies — creator safety, active harm, medical crisis, doxxing incident in progress.

**6.1. Immediate life-safety emergency.** Contact local emergency services (Colombia: 123). PNPtv! is not equipped to substitute for emergency responders.

**6.2. Safety incident that is not life-threatening but urgent** (active doxxing, credible violent threat, impersonation attempt, coordinated harassment campaign):

- Post immediately to Slack DM to Santino (contact stored in Slack profile). SMS/phone reserved for this scenario only.
- Post also to Slack `#ext-[handle]` so it is on the record.
- Cristina AI Slackbot has an escalation routine that pages the on-call ops person; message `@cristina help` in any Slack channel to trigger it.

**6.3. Non-urgent but sensitive** (mental-health support, personal crisis unrelated to platform):

- Reference the **PNPtv! Creator Care resources** in `/docs/creator-care-program-en.html` (`creator-care-program-es.html` in Spanish).
- You are welcome to note in Slack `#ext-[handle]` that you need reduced availability for a period; ops will pause your discovery placements and communicate to booked members on your behalf.

## 7. Public Statements About PNPtv!

**7.1. Promotional statements.** You are free — and encouraged — to promote the platform on your social media, in interviews, at events, and in personal conversation. Use of the PNPtv! logo and brand for this purpose is granted under the Partner Agreement (Document 2 § 4).

**7.2. Speaking for PNPtv!** You are **not** authorized to speak on behalf of PNPtv! — to press, to other platforms, to regulators, to industry organizations, to law enforcement — without prior written approval from Santino.

- Journalist reaches out asking about a PNPtv! policy? Refer them to `press@pnptv.app`.
- Another platform asks you to comment on PNPtv!'s approach? Tell them to email `press@pnptv.app`.
- Law enforcement contact requesting information about PNPtv! or a specific user? Do not respond substantively; refer to `legal@pnptv.app` and tell ops in Slack immediately.

**7.3. Your own opinions.** You remain free to express your own opinions publicly, including opinions critical of PNPtv! — subject to (a) the internal-escalation-first rule in Document 4 § 3.5, and (b) not disclosing confidential information covered by the NDA.

## 8. Confidentiality Reminder

**8.1.** Internal Slack, `#ext-[handle]` content, and the Partner Program Telegram group are **confidential** under the NDA (Document 1). Do not screenshot and post to social media.

**8.2.** Aggregate platform metrics, roadmap information disclosed in `#creator-announcements` or preview form, other creators' identities beyond public creator handles, member PII, and internal ops procedures are all confidential.

**8.3.** You may share your **own** experience in general terms (e.g., "I'm doing a callback for PNPtv! this week"). You may not share content that identifies other creators or members, or that quotes internal Slack discussions.

## 9. Enforcement

**9.1. Ladder.** Off-channel communication about platform business is enforced under the ladder in **Document 4 § 5**:

- Isolated first instance, minor topic → verbal coaching (Step 1).
- Repeat, or first instance involving member solicitation off-channel → written warning (Step 2).
- Pattern of off-channel platform work, or single instance involving financial diversion → suspension (Step 3), which for Document 7 (Anti-Poaching) violations may skip directly here.
- Ongoing pattern after suspension → termination for cause (Step 4).

**9.2. Distinct violations.** Off-channel solicitation with financial diversion also violates **Document 7 (Anti-Poaching & Non-Circumvention Policy)** and escalates on that separate schedule, which is faster than the standard ladder.

**9.3. Grace for good-faith mistakes.** New creators occasionally get a WhatsApp from a member and reply once before catching themselves. First occurrence with a good-faith explanation and immediate correction (redirect using the Section 4 template) is a coaching moment, not a warning. Repeated occurrences are not.

## 10. Scope and Amendment

**10.1.** This Policy binds all creators in the Partner Program.

**10.2.** PNPtv! may amend this Policy with **14 days' notice** in Slack `#creator-announcements`.

**10.3.** In case of conflict with the Code of Conduct (Document 4), Telegram Group Guidelines (Document 6), or Anti-Poaching Policy (Document 7): each of those documents controls within its own subject matter; this Policy controls the channel-choice question.
