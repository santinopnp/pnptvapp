# PNPtv! Anti-Poaching & Non-Circumvention Policy

**Policy Document 7 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.** This is a policy, not a per-signature contract. Creators agree to it by joining the Partner Program; the Partner Agreement (Document 2) incorporates it by reference and its enforcement mechanics — including retroactive revenue adjustment and liquidated damages — flow through Documents 2 and 4.

---

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

Esta Política prohíbe **desviar miembros de PNPtv! fuera de la plataforma** para transacciones que deberían ser pagadas dentro. PNPtv! invierte activamente en atraer a cada miembro (marketing, referidos, atención al miembro, hosting, cumplimiento 2257, procesamiento de pagos). Cuando un Creador redirige a un miembro a OnlyFans, PayPal, Zelle, Cash App, transferencia directa de crypto, o cualquier otro canal donde PNPtv! no participa, es **desviación directa de ingresos** — no un "ahorro de comisión" — y viola el marco de confianza del Programa de Socios.

**Prohibido**: dirigir miembros a plataformas o métodos de pago externos, publicitar tus tarifas en otras plataformas dirigidas a miembros de PNPtv!, intercambiar contactos personales por servicios pagados fuera de plataforma, reclutar a otros Creadores de PNPtv! a una plataforma competidora (durante la participación + **12 meses** posteriores), reclutar personal. **Permitido**: estar presente en otras plataformas (PNPtv! es **no exclusivo**), promocionar tu presencia de PNPtv! desde esas plataformas, redes sociales públicas para tu marca general, amistades personales genuinas independientes de arreglos financieros. **Escalada**: (1) advertencia escrita + ajuste retroactivo de reparto de ingresos por la fuga detectada; (2) suspensión de 30 días + congelamiento de ganancias; (3) terminación por causa justa + daños liquidados por Acuerdo de Socios + potencial acción legal. **Cola de no-solicitación** de miembros y otros Creadores continúa **12 meses** después de la terminación.

## 1. Rationale

**1.1.** PNPtv! actively invests in acquiring, retaining, and caring for each member: paid marketing, referral rewards, member-care operations (Cristina AI + human support), platform hosting and delivery infrastructure, 2257 compliance systems, payment processing, dispute handling, chargeback defense.

**1.2.** When a Creator redirects a member to a channel where PNPtv! does not participate, it is not a "fee-avoidance courtesy" to the member — it is **direct diversion of revenue** the platform earned by acquiring and serving that member. It also strips the member of platform protections (2257 compliance, refund/dispute mechanisms, moderation, safety features) and strips the Creator of platform protections (payment guarantees, chargeback defense, ID-verified counterparties, escalation resources).

**1.3.** The 70/30 revenue split assumes members flow through the platform. Off-platform diversion breaks that assumption, breaks the trust of Creators who play by the rules and depend on the platform's continued investment, and is treated as a serious breach.

**1.4.** This Policy is intentionally narrower than a non-compete. PNPtv! is **non-exclusive** — Creators are actively encouraged to build presence on other platforms. What is prohibited is the specific act of **moving a member acquired through PNPtv! to a channel where PNPtv! is not paid**.

## 2. What is PROHIBITED — Poaching & Circumvention

### 2.1. Off-platform payment diversion.

Directing a PNPtv! member to pay you for services via:

- OnlyFans, Fansly, JustForFans, Faphouse, Fanvue, or any other adult creator platform;
- PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, Wise, Revolut, or any other peer-to-peer payment service;
- Direct crypto wallet transfer (Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum, Solana, USDC, any other) outside the PNPtv! payment flow;
- Bank transfer, wire transfer, ACH;
- Gift cards, Amazon wishlists, Throne / Bad Dragon / third-party wishlist services **when solicited from a PNPtv! member as an alternative to on-platform tipping** (a passive wishlist link on your public social media is not the same thing — see Section 3.2);
- Any other channel where PNPtv! is not the counterparty and does not receive its revenue share.

### 2.2. Off-platform advertising to PNPtv! members.

Posting on other platforms in a way that specifically targets PNPtv! members and pitches those members to move their spending off PNPtv!. Examples:

- "PNPtv! subscribers — I do custom [X] on my other page for [Y]% less."
- A callout post naming PNPtv! members by handle and inviting them to a rival service.
- DMing PNPtv! members on Instagram / X / Twitter after obtaining their off-platform contact through PNPtv! to pitch competitor services.

### 2.3. Contact-swap for paid off-platform services.

Trading a personal WhatsApp number, personal Instagram handle, personal Telegram, personal email, or any other personal contact info with a PNPtv! member so that the member can pay the Creator for services outside the platform. This is the most common form of circumvention and is easy to detect via member surveys and chargeback patterns.

### 2.4. "Avoid the platform fee" framing.

Any communication with a member that positions off-platform payment as a way to save the platform's cut ("we can skip the fees if you just Zelle me"). This is the exact behavior being prohibited and is treated as an admission that Section 2.1 applies.

### 2.5. Recruiting other PNPtv! Creators to a competing platform.

During your Partner Program participation and for **12 months after termination**:

- Do not solicit, encourage, or facilitate other PNPtv! Creators to leave PNPtv! for a specific competing platform.
- Do not offer bounties, splits, or referral kickbacks to move other PNPtv! Creators to a competing platform.
- Neutral factual information ("[Competitor] just changed their payout schedule") is fine; targeted recruitment is not.

### 2.6. Recruiting PNPtv! staff.

During your Partner Program participation and for **12 months after termination**, do not solicit PNPtv! staff (Santino, Carlos, Miguel, contract moderators, contract engineers, Cristina AI operators, member-care team) for employment or contract engagement with a competing platform or a business you are affiliated with.

### 2.7. Using PNPtv! discovery, member data, or platform intelligence to bootstrap off-platform outreach.

- Downloading, exporting, screenshotting, or transcribing member lists.
- Using the platform's member-facing tools (DMs, tip records, subscription lists) as source data for a competing-platform mailing list.
- Selling or trading PNPtv! member contact information to any third party.
- This is also a Document 1 NDA breach and escalates faster.

## 3. What IS ALLOWED — Explicit Safe Harbors

**3.1. Multi-platform presence.**

PNPtv! is **non-exclusive**. You may operate on OnlyFans, Fansly, JustForFans, Fanvue, any other adult platform, or independently. You may earn from those platforms in parallel with PNPtv!. There is no requirement to disclose your presence on other platforms.

**3.2. Cross-promoting your PNPtv! presence FROM other platforms.**

- Posting on your OnlyFans / Instagram / X / TikTok that you're going live on PNPtv! — encouraged.
- Linking to your PNPtv! profile from other platforms — encouraged.
- Passive wishlists, Throne links, tip jars on your public social profiles — allowed, provided you do not specifically pitch them to PNPtv! members as an alternative to on-platform tipping.

**3.3. Public social media for general brand.**

- Twitter/X, TikTok, general social presence promoting your brand, aesthetic, and independent projects — all permitted.
- Discussing your work generally, your industry views, your creative projects — all permitted, subject to NDA and to Document 4 § 3.5 (no public complaint about PNPtv! without internal escalation first).

**3.4. Personal friendships.**

- Friendships that predate your Partner Program membership and existed independently of a paid PNPtv! transaction are unaffected.
- Friendships that emerge organically without a financial-services arrangement are unaffected.
- The line is whether the relationship is a business relationship. Real friendship + no money changing hands off-platform for services = allowed. Business dressed as friendship = poaching.

**3.5. Servicing a member you introduced to PNPtv! before joining.**

- If a member was already a paying customer of yours on another platform, and you invited them to PNPtv! after joining the Partner Program (tracked via the referral system if you used a referral link, or otherwise attestable), you are not required to stop serving that member off-platform in the pre-existing off-platform context.
- **However**: that member's PNPtv! activity — their PNPtv! subscription, tips, private-call bookings, VOD purchases, all engagement inside the platform — remains PNPtv! revenue. You cannot pull their PNPtv! spending off-platform.
- If a member you referred prefers to interact with you only via PNPtv!, they belong on PNPtv!.

**3.6. Ambiguous situations — the safe path.**

- If you are unsure whether an interaction crosses the line, **ask in Slack `#ext-[handle]` before acting**.
- Asking is never held against you. Acting first and asking after — when the answer would have been "no, don't" — is held against you.

## 4. Detection

PNPtv! detects circumvention through:

- **Member surveys and exit interviews** — members are periodically asked how their interactions with creators are going, including whether creators have ever asked them to move off-platform.
- **Chargeback patterns** — chargebacks that trace to "I paid but got told to send more via Zelle" or "the creator now only responds off-platform" surface directly.
- **Direct member reports** — members reporting solicitation attempts via the in-platform `/report` flow.
- **Review of platform messages** — automated moderation of on-platform DMs and human moderation of flagged conversations, per the platform Terms of Service. This is a moderation function, not surveillance of creators specifically.
- **Industry intelligence** — screenshots and reports from other creators, from members, from ops's own monitoring of public creator social presence.
- **Payment reconciliation gaps** — patterns where a creator's discovery traffic and DM engagement are high but on-platform monetization is disproportionately low often indicate diversion.

Detection is not surveillance for surveillance's sake. It is enforcement of the trust framework so that creators who play by the rules are not competing against creators who don't.

## 5. Enforcement

Anti-Poaching enforcement is on a **faster ladder** than the standard Code of Conduct ladder because the harm is measurable financial diversion, not a cultural infraction.

**5.1. First offense — Written warning + retroactive revenue-share adjustment.**

- Formal written warning delivered in Slack `#ext-[handle]`.
- Retroactive **revenue-share adjustment** for the detected leakage: PNPtv! calculates a good-faith estimate of the revenue that would have flowed through the platform absent the diversion, applies the standard 30% platform share to that amount, and offsets it against the Creator's upcoming payout.
- Creator has 14 days to dispute the calculation in writing; ops reviews and either sustains, adjusts, or reverses the offset. Sustained offsets deduct from the next payout batch, with the balance carried forward if the batch is insufficient.
- Behavior change expected within 30 days.

**5.2. Second offense — Suspension (30 days) + earnings freeze pending review.**

- Platform access suspended for 30 days.
- All pending Creator earnings are **frozen** pending completion of a review — not confiscated, frozen. Standing on other policies is also reviewed.
- Ops conducts a review within the 30-day window: interviews (voluntary), payment records, on-platform DM audit for the affected members, member survey outreach.
- Outcome:
  - Reinstatement with a corrective plan and a second retroactive revenue-share adjustment for any newly-detected leakage; or
  - Extended suspension; or
  - Progression to Step 3.

**5.3. Third offense OR clear intent OR a single egregious incident — Termination for cause + liquidated damages + potential legal action.**

- Termination for cause under Partner Agreement (Document 2) § 14.
- **Liquidated damages** per the schedule in the Partner Agreement (Document 2 § 16, "Liquidated Damages / Cláusula Penal") — currently placeholder pending Colombian legal counsel review; anticipated to be defined as a multiple of the estimated diverted revenue over the relevant lookback window. The specific formula is set in Document 2 § 16 and will be finalized on legal review. The Non-Circumvention obligation itself is set in Document 2 § 12; the liability cap in § 15 does NOT shield Non-Circumvention breaches (see § 15 carve-out).
- **Potential legal action** — PNPtv! reserves the right to pursue damages, injunctive relief, and any other remedies available under Colombian law. Non-solicitation clauses in service contracts are enforceable in Colombia subject to reasonableness of scope and duration, which this Policy is designed to satisfy (limited to specific enumerated behaviors, geographic scope tied to platform reach, temporal scope of participation + 12 months).

**5.4. Escalation shortcut.**

- A single incident involving (i) an itemized "invoice" solicited off-platform for a specific act, (ii) a coordinated campaign to move multiple members off-platform simultaneously, or (iii) member PII export to a competitor platform, may skip directly to Step 2 or Step 3 depending on scale.
- Determination is made by Santino (or, if unavailable, by Miguel with Santino's written concurrence within 72 hours).

**5.5. Reset.**

- A first-offense written warning older than **12 months** with no repeat behavior in that period rolls off the anti-poaching ladder for progression purposes. It remains in the file for context.
- Suspension and termination outcomes do not reset — they remain on the file permanently.

**5.6. Appeals.**

- Step 1 (warning + offset): appeal within 14 days in writing to Santino. Ops responds within 14 days.
- Step 2 (suspension): appeal within 14 days in writing to Santino, or to `oversight@pnptv.app` (external adviser channel, per Document 4 § 6.3) if the Creator prefers independent review. External-adviser review is nonbinding but weighed heavily by ops.
- Step 3 (termination): the Partner Agreement § 14 due-process rules apply — written notice, opportunity to respond, ops review, final written decision.

## 6. Tail Period — What Continues After the Partner Program Ends

**6.1.** Certain obligations survive termination of Partner Program participation for a **tail period of 12 months** following the termination date.

**6.2. Surviving obligations.**

- **Non-solicitation of PNPtv! members.** For 12 months after termination, you may not solicit members you first interacted with via PNPtv! for competing services. You may continue to serve those members in any pre-existing off-platform context that predated your Partner Program participation (per Section 3.5).
- **Non-solicitation of other PNPtv! Creators.** For 12 months, you may not recruit or facilitate recruitment of other PNPtv! Creators to a competing platform per Section 2.5.
- **Non-solicitation of PNPtv! staff.** For 12 months, you may not solicit PNPtv! staff to a competing engagement per Section 2.6.
- **Confidentiality.** NDA obligations (Document 1) survive per Document 1's own terms, independent of this Policy.

**6.3. What does NOT survive.**

- You may continue to operate on other platforms without limitation.
- You may publicly discuss having previously been a PNPtv! Creator.
- You may serve members who independently seek you out on other platforms after termination without having been recruited by you in violation of § 6.2 — a member's own choice to find you elsewhere is not solicitation by you.

**6.4. Duration is a judgment call.**

- 12 months is the working number based on industry norms for creator-platform non-solicitation clauses (12–24 months is a common range). It is subject to Colombian counsel review for enforceability; a shorter period may result.

## 7. Members Introduced BY the Creator

**7.1.** When a Creator brings a member into PNPtv! from the Creator's own audience — tracked via the referral system, or otherwise attestable at the time of onboarding — the Creator is:

- **Not prohibited** from continuing to serve that member off-platform in a pre-existing off-platform context (an established OnlyFans / Fansly customer, an established custom-content client, an established personal off-platform relationship).
- **Not entitled** to move that member's PNPtv!-specific activity off-platform. Once the member is spending on PNPtv! — subscribing, tipping, buying VOD, booking private calls — that spending is PNPtv! revenue and remains PNPtv! revenue.

**7.2.** The distinction is:

- Off-platform activity that was already happening before the referral: continues, unaffected.
- On-platform activity that emerged from the referral: stays on-platform.

**7.3.** Referral tracking.

- Creators are encouraged to use the platform's referral link system for onboarding their own audience.
- Referral status is one factor considered when evaluating whether a member is "your" pre-existing customer vs. a platform-acquired member. It is not the only factor — attestation and consistent evidence (past invoices, prior chat history, mutual acknowledgment) can substitute.

## 8. Interaction with Other Policies

**8.1.** This Policy and the Communications Policy (Document 5) overlap where off-platform communication is the vehicle for off-platform payment. In such cases, both policies apply; the enforcement outcome is whichever ladder is stricter (usually Document 7).

**8.2.** This Policy and the Code of Conduct (Document 4) overlap where recruitment of other Creators becomes drama or gossip. Same principle: whichever ladder is stricter.

**8.3.** This Policy is subordinate to the Partner Agreement (Document 2) — Document 2 controls on termination mechanics, payout offsets, liquidated damages formulas, and governing-law questions.

**8.4.** Nothing in this Policy overrides the non-exclusivity language in Document 2 § 3.2(e) — Creators remain free to work with third parties, subject only to the specific enumerated restrictions above.

---

## 9. Scope and Amendment

**9.1.** This Policy binds all Creators in the Partner Program.

**9.2.** PNPtv! may amend this Policy with **14 days' notice** posted in Slack `#creator-announcements` and emailed to the address on file. Amendments that lengthen the tail period, expand prohibited channels, or tighten enforcement receive **30 days' notice** and are called out in the announcement subject line.

**9.3.** No retroactive application of amendments. Amendments apply prospectively from the effective date. Behavior that occurred before an amendment is judged by the Policy in force at the time of the behavior.
