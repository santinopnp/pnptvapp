# PNPtv! Creator Content License Addendum

**Document 3 of 3 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.**

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

Este Anexo de Licencia de Contenido complementa el **Acuerdo del Programa de Socios Creadores** (Documento 2) y define exactamente qué derechos otorga el Creador a PNPtv! sobre el contenido que publica en la plataforma. El Creador **conserva la titularidad** de su contenido y otorga a PNPtv! una **licencia no exclusiva, mundial, libre de regalías** para reproducir, transmitir, distribuir y mostrar el contenido en todas las superficies de la plataforma (streams en vivo, VOD/PNP Channels, hangouts, llamadas privadas, DMs, Main Stage y clips promocionales de hasta 60 segundos). PNPtv! puede transcodificar, generar miniaturas y clips promocionales derivados. Los **derechos morales** del Creador se respetan: PNPtv! muestra el handle del Creador en el contenido destacado. Tras la terminación: los clips promocionales ya publicados pueden permanecer en línea **90 días**; el contenido completo se dará de baja en **30 días** desde la solicitud del Creador. El Creador **puede distribuir su propio contenido en otras plataformas** (licencia no exclusiva). PNPtv! se compromete a **enviar avisos DMCA** y sus equivalentes colombianos cuando el contenido del Creador sea pirateado; el Creador se compromete a no subir contenido de otros creadores pirateado. Se rige por la ley colombiana; disputas por **arbitraje en la Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá D.C.**

## 3. Grant of License

**3.1.** Subject to the terms of this Addendum, the Creator grants PNPtv! a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable (to service providers acting on PNPtv!'s behalf) license**, during the Term (as defined in Section 6) and for the post-term promotional window in Section 6.2, to:

- **(a)** **Reproduce** the Content on PNPtv!'s servers, storage systems (including third-party cloud providers used for hosting, backup, and CDN delivery), and end-user devices;
- **(b)** **Transmit and distribute** the Content across all platform surfaces, including:
  - live streams (real-time and recorded rebroadcasts),
  - VOD on **PNP Channels**,
  - group video hangouts,
  - private video calls,
  - direct messages,
  - the **Main Stage** broadcast surface,
  - notification previews (thumbnails and short previews delivered by push notification or in-app), and
  - any additional platform surface PNPtv! may launch in the future consistent with the Creator's Content Standards permissions;
- **(c)** **Publicly perform and display** the Content on those surfaces to the platform's audience;
- **(d)** **Cache** the Content on CDN edge nodes for delivery purposes;
- **(e)** **Encode, transcode, and adaptively re-encode** the Content into the file formats, resolutions, bitrates, and codecs required for delivery to a variety of devices and networks;
- **(f)** **Generate thumbnails, previews, and short clips** from the Content, including automated frame extraction, waveform previews, and AI-assisted still/preview generation;
- **(g)** **Create promotional derivative excerpts** of up to **sixty (60) seconds** in length for use in promotional materials, including "coming up on PNPtv!" carousels, "best-of" reels, in-app promotional banners, and outbound social-media amplification via approved channels;
- **(h)** **Include the Content in aggregate promotional materials** that combine content from multiple Creators (e.g., seasonal highlight reels or category showcases); and
- **(i)** **Display the Creator's handle, avatar, and public profile information** in connection with the Content as reasonably necessary to identify the Creator.

**3.2.** **Approved social channels.** As of the Effective Date, PNPtv!'s approved external social channels for amplification are: (i) X (formerly Twitter); (ii) PNPtv!-operated newsletter; (iii) other channels expressly designated in writing by PNPtv! from time to time. **PNPtv! does not operate on Bluesky or Discord** and this Addendum does not authorize distribution to those platforms.

**3.3.** **No hidden uses.** Beyond the uses expressly enumerated in this Section 3, PNPtv! shall not commercially exploit the Content in materially different ways (for example, licensing the Content to unrelated third parties for stand-alone distribution) without the Creator's prior written consent.

## 4. Moral Rights and Attribution

**4.1. Attribution.** PNPtv! agrees to **display the Creator's handle** on all featured Content in a manner reasonably visible to viewers, consistent with the platform's UI conventions. Featured Content includes VOD listings, live-stream cards, hangout listings, promotional clips, and cross-posts.

**4.2. Right of Integrity.** PNPtv! will not intentionally modify the substantive artistic content of the Content in a manner that misrepresents the Creator or would be reasonably offensive to the Creator. Purely technical modifications (transcoding, thumbnailing, clipping to 60-second promotional excerpts, adding platform watermarks) are permitted and are not considered violations of integrity.

**4.3. Colombian Moral Rights.** The Parties acknowledge that under Colombian copyright law (Ley 23 de 1982 and related provisions), moral rights are inalienable. Nothing in this Addendum is intended to constitute a waiver of the Creator's moral rights beyond the specific consents given in this Section 4.

## 5. Non-Exclusivity — Creator May Distribute Elsewhere

**5.1.** The license granted in Section 3 is **non-exclusive**. The Creator expressly retains the right to reproduce, transmit, distribute, publicly perform, display, and otherwise exploit the Creator's own Content on any other platform, service, or medium, on any terms the Creator sees fit, subject only to:

- **(a)** the Non-Circumvention obligation in Section 12 of the Partner Agreement (which restricts using PNPtv!-obtained member PII to solicit those members off-platform, not fair independent distribution); and
- **(b)** any confidentiality obligation in the NDA (Document 1) that would restrict disclosure of PNPtv!-specific Content or information (e.g., internal roadmap references baked into a stream shall not be re-published elsewhere).

## 6. Term and Post-Termination

**6.1. Term.** This Addendum begins on the Effective Date of the Partner Agreement and continues while the Partner Agreement remains in effect (the **"Term"**).

**6.2. Post-Term Promotional Window.** For **twelve (12) months** after the effective date of termination of the Partner Agreement, PNPtv! retains a limited license to continue using **already-created promotional derivative excerpts** (up to 60 seconds each) featuring the Content in promotional materials, provided PNPtv! ceases creating new promotional derivatives from the Creator's Content on the termination date.

**6.3. Post-Termination Deprecation of Full Content.**

- **(a)** **Live surfaces.** PNPtv! shall cease publishing the Creator's full-length Content (VOD, long-form recordings, hangout archives) to public discovery surfaces within **thirty (30) days** of the Creator's written termination request (or, if PNPtv! initiates termination for cause under Section 13.4 of the Partner Agreement, within thirty (30) days of the termination effective date).
- **(b)** **Promotional clips grace period.** Promotional clips already published and in circulation may remain live for the ninety (90)-day period described in Section 6.4 below, after which they shall be deprecated absent extension by mutual agreement.
- **(c)** **Backups and archival copies.** PNPtv! may retain encrypted archival copies of the Content for (i) legal-hold, regulatory-compliance, or 2257-recordkeeping obligations; (ii) chargeback and dispute defense; and (iii) routine, non-targeted backup cycles until overwritten in the ordinary course. Such archival copies shall not be re-published.

**6.4. Promotional Clips Grace Period.** Promotional clips of up to 60 seconds that were already **published** as of the termination date may remain live for **ninety (90) days** following termination, after which PNPtv! shall take them down.

**6.5. Creator Data Export.** For **ninety (90) days** following termination, PNPtv! shall make available to the Creator a data-export tool through which the Creator may download the Creator's own uploaded Content, as further described in Section 13.6(a) of the Partner Agreement.

**6.6. Immediate Takedown for Cause.** Notwithstanding Sections 6.3 and 6.4, PNPtv! shall take down Content **immediately** (with reasonable technical latency) on becoming aware that the Content:

- **(a)** violates any absolute prohibition under Section 8.2(b) of the Partner Agreement (e.g., depiction of a minor, non-consent, etc.);
- **(b)** is subject to a valid DMCA takedown or Colombian equivalent by a rights-holder other than the Creator; or
- **(c)** is the subject of a court order or governmental request compelling removal.

## 7. Anti-Piracy

**7.1. PNPtv!'s Commitments.** PNPtv! shall, at no additional charge to the Creator during the Term:

- **(a)** issue DMCA takedown notices under 17 U.S.C. § 512 to hosting providers, search engines, and social platforms when the Creator's Content, subject to the Creator's active license to PNPtv!, is identified on third-party sites without authorization;
- **(b)** issue equivalent takedown notices under Colombian law, including invocations of Ley 23 de 1982 (Derechos de Autor) and Ley 1915 de 2018 (Modernización del Derecho de Autor) where applicable; and
- **(c)** provide the Creator with reasonable evidentiary support (upload logs, hash records, timestamps) to substantiate the Creator's rights in the event a DMCA counter-notice or dispute arises.

**7.2. Scope of Anti-Piracy Support.**

- **(a)** PNPtv!'s DMCA-support obligation is limited to Content **that was uploaded to or streamed through PNPtv!** and that remains under a live license to PNPtv! at the time of the takedown request.
- **(b)** PNPtv! does not guarantee that any particular pirate site will comply, and PNPtv! is not obligated to litigate claims on the Creator's behalf. On material or repeat infringement, PNPtv! and the Creator will discuss escalation.

**7.3. Creator Anti-Piracy Obligations.** The Creator warrants and covenants that the Creator shall not knowingly:

- **(a)** upload, stream, or otherwise make available on the platform any content owned or created by **any other creator or performer** (whether or not that creator is on PNPtv!) without that creator's express written permission; and
- **(b)** solicit, encourage, or participate in the piracy or unauthorized redistribution of other PNPtv! Creators' Content.

**7.4.** Breach of Section 7.3 is grounds for termination for cause under Section 13.4 of the Partner Agreement.
