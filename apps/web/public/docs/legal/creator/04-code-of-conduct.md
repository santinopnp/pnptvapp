**Policy Document 4 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.** This is a policy, not a per-signature contract. Creators agree to it by joining the Partner Program; the Partner Agreement (Document 2) incorporates it by reference.

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

PNPtv! es una plataforma profesional para adultos que sirve a una comunidad históricamente marginada. Como Creador del Programa de Socios, tú eres la cara pública de esa comunidad — el estándar es **profesional, digno y basado en la reducción de daños**. No juzgamos el estilo de vida PNP; lo protegemos.

Este Código enumera dos tipos de conducta: (1) **tolerancia cero** — comportamientos que resultan en suspensión inmediata o terminación por causa justa (contenido con menores, no-consentimiento, doxxing, discurso de odio, acoso sexual, venta de sustancias ilegales, evasión de 2257, etc.); y (2) **conducta que activa la escalera de sanciones** — drama público, hablar mal de otros creadores, quejarse de miembros o de la plataforma en público, cazar suscriptores de otros creadores, no presentarse a streams o llamadas privadas, y responder tarde a Slack.

La **escalera de sanciones** es consistente en los cuatro documentos de política: (1) coaching verbal → (2) advertencia escrita → (3) suspensión con congelamiento de ganancias durante la investigación → (4) terminación por causa justa con debido proceso. Al participar en el Programa de Socios aceptas este Código.

## 1. Purpose and Standard

**1.1.** PNPtv! serves the queer PNP (party & play) community — a group that has been marginalized, criminalized, moralized at, and abandoned by mainstream platforms. The Partner Program exists to give creators from this community a **safe, dignified, professionally-run place to work**.

**1.2.** As a Creator you are the public face of that mission. The standard is **professional, harm-reduction-aware, consent-first, and welfare-first**. We do not judge the PNP lifestyle. We do not moralize at members. We are not a rehabilitation program. We are a safe haven staffed by people who understand.

**1.3.** This Code applies to your conduct:

- **(a)** on the PNPtv! platform (streams, hangouts, private calls, DMs, Main Stage, tips chat);
- **(b)** in Partner Program communication channels (Slack workspace, Partner Program Telegram group);
- **(c)** in public and semi-public settings where you are identifiable as a PNPtv! Creator (personal social media, other adult platforms where you promote your PNPtv! presence, industry events).

**1.4.** This Code is a **standard of professional practice**, not employment supervision. You remain an independent contractor. PNPtv! does not direct when, where, or how you work; it enforces the Code by suspending or terminating access to the Partner Program when the standard is not met.

## 2. Zero-Tolerance Behaviors — Immediate Suspension or Termination for Cause

The following are grounds for **immediate suspension of platform access and earnings**, pending a rapid investigation, and typically result in **termination for cause** under Section 14 of the Partner Agreement:

**2.1. Content involving minors.** Any depiction of a person under 18 (or apparent under-18), whether real, simulated, drawn, AI-generated, or roleplayed. This is a criminal-law red line and there is no appeal.

**2.2. Non-consensual content or conduct.** Depictions or facilitation of non-consensual sexual acts. Sharing intimate images without consent. Coercion, threats, or manipulation to obtain content or acts from members, other creators, or third parties.

**2.3. Bestiality.**

**2.4. Explicit drug-sale transactions.** Using the platform, DMs, or comms channels to sell, arrange the sale of, or connect buyers to sellers of illegal substances. Discussing PNP substances in a **harm-reduction context** is not a violation (see Section 4.3). Facilitating a transaction is.

**2.5. Doxxing or outing.** Publishing, threatening to publish, or trading in the following about members, other creators, or staff without consent:

- legal name;
- home address, workplace, employer, school;
- HIV/HCV/STI status or other medical information;
- immigration status;
- family relationships;
- other private identifying information.

Outing someone's PNP participation, sexual orientation, gender identity, or serostatus without consent is doxxing.

**2.6. Hate speech / hateful conduct.** Racism, transphobia, biphobia, homophobia, femmephobia, serophobia (HIV-status stigma), ableism, xenophobia, antisemitism, Islamophobia, ageism against elders in the community, body-shaming as slurs, or hate speech targeting any protected class. Slurs, symbols, stereotypes, or dehumanizing framing directed at protected groups. Judged on impact, not intent — "it was a joke" is not a defense.

**2.7. Sexual harassment, coercion, or grooming of members.** Ignoring stated limits, escalating past a consent boundary, pressuring a member for content or acts they have declined, exploiting a member's vulnerability (isolation, intoxication, crisis) for financial or sexual gain.

**2.8. Violence, threats, or intimidation.** Threats of physical harm, threats of doxxing, threats of legal weaponization, credible intimidation directed at any member of the community — members, creators, staff, or their families.

**2.9. Identity fraud beyond persona.** Persona is welcome (stage names, characters, fictional backstories). **Identity fraud** — impersonating a real person to deceive members financially or emotionally, or catfishing in a way that would ground a member's reliance on a materially false identity for money — is not.

**2.10. Providing or selling illegal substances via the platform.** Includes on-stream sales, "come by my hotel" arrangements initiated through platform DMs, or listing a "menu" of substances. This is separate from Section 2.4 which covers the specific act of a sale transaction; this covers holding out the platform as a marketplace.

**2.11. Circumventing 2257.** Featuring an unverified performer in your content, misrepresenting a performer's age, submitting altered or forged IDs, coaching a performer to defeat the verification flow.

**2.12. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter.** See Section 6.4.

**Enforcement for Section 2 violations:** Suspension is immediate on credible report. Investigation is conducted by PNPtv! ops (Santino, Miguel, or designee) within **7 calendar days**. Creator is given the opportunity to respond in writing. Outcome ranges from reinstatement to termination for cause. In cases involving Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, or 2.10, PNPtv! may also report to law enforcement as required by law.

## 3. Behaviors That Trigger the Warning Ladder

These behaviors do not, on a first occurrence, end your participation in the Partner Program. They do trigger the **enforcement ladder in Section 5**. A pattern of these behaviors is grounds for termination.

### 3.1. Drama and public grievance-airing.

- **No public airing of grievances** about other creators, members, or the platform on your personal social media, on-stream, in hangouts, or in the Partner Program Telegram group.
- Concerns go to Slack `#ext-[handle]` (private) or Slack `#creator-support` (peer + ops), per the Communications Policy (Document 5).
- Subtweeting other creators, "vaguebooking" about members, and "someone on this platform..." posts are drama. Don't.

### 3.2. Trash-talking competitor creators.

- Bad-mouthing another PNPtv! creator on-platform, in the Partner Program Telegram group, or on external social media is not our brand.
- This includes comparing rates, comparing bodies, comparing traffic, comparing scandals, or characterizing another creator as "unprofessional," "fake," etc. in public.

### 3.3. Passive-aggressive commentary.

- Sideways posts about other creators' work, streams, earnings, tips, or member base. If you have a specific concern about a specific creator, escalate privately per Section 6.

### 3.4. Complaining about members publicly.

- Members are the reason the platform exists. Even difficult members.
- **Bad-actor members are reported via** the in-platform `/report` flow or a Slack post to `#creator-support` — never dragged in public, never named on-stream, never subtweeted.
- "Ugh why do people even..." posts about member behavior are a violation.

### 3.5. Complaining about the platform publicly before escalating internally.

- Bugs, slow payouts, discovery-algorithm frustration, feature gaps — all legitimate concerns. **Escalation path is internal first**: Slack `#creator-support` (public but professional), Slack `#ext-[handle]` (private with your ops contact), or DM to Santino / Miguel.
- Public complaint (Twitter/X, other platforms, industry press) about platform issues **before** internal escalation, or public complaint about internal responses without permission, is a breach.
- Post-resolution: if you're still dissatisfied and want to speak publicly, tell your ops contact first. We would rather hear "I'm about to post about this" than read it.

### 3.6. Poaching other creators' regulars.

- No aggressive DMs, tip-baiting, "come to my stream instead" campaigns, or coordinated push at another creator's known regular members.
- Compete on quality of your own product, not on sabotage. Collab and cross-promotion are welcome (Section 4.5).

### 3.8. No-showing scheduled streams or booked private calls without notice.

- You set your own schedule. When you commit to a slot — a booked private call, a scheduled stream in the discovery grid, a hangout appearance — you show up or you cancel with reasonable notice.
- **Reasonable notice**: 2 hours for a booked private call; 30 minutes for a scheduled stream if genuinely unavoidable.
- Repeated no-shows damage member trust and platform reputation and are grounds for escalation.

### 3.9. Late responses to Slack `#ext-[handle]` beyond SLA.

- The SLA in the Communications Policy (Document 5) is **12 business hours** during your stated working hours for acknowledgment. Not resolution — acknowledgment.
- Chronically ignoring your ops channel is a signal you are not participating in the Partner Program in good faith and triggers the ladder.

### 3.10. Screenshotting internal Slack or Partner Program Telegram content to external audiences.

- Also an NDA (Document 1) breach. Handled under this Code as a warning-ladder item when the disclosure is minor; escalates to termination for cause when disclosure is material (member PII, financials, unreleased product info).

## 4. Positive Standards — What We Expect From You

These are not enforced by suspension. They are the working culture and are used in performance reviews, tier decisions, promotional slot allocations, and Wall of Fame selection.

### 4.1. Professionalism on-stream and in DMs.

- Show up on time, camera framed, lighting decent, audio clear enough for the format.
- In DMs: respond in a reasonable timeframe, honor stated prices, don't ghost paying members.
- If you're having a rough day and can't perform, cancel and reschedule — don't push through and give a bad member experience.

### 4.2. Consent-first culture with members.

- **Verbal consent for private-call activities.** Members state what they want; you confirm what you'll do and what your limits are. Both sides can revoke consent at any time.
- **Respect stated member limits.** No means no on the first ask.
- **Don't push consent through financial pressure.** "Tip more and I'll..." is fine as a tip menu. "You already tipped, so now you have to..." is not.

### 4.3. Harm-reduction messaging in PNP-related content.

- You are not required to talk about substances. If you do, PNPtv!'s house standard is **harm reduction, not encouragement, not moralization**.
- **Never encourage** unsafe combinations (e.g., stimulants + GHB + poppers + PDE5i stacks), unsafe injection practices, or sharing needles.
- If a member indicates active crisis, overdose risk, medical emergency, suicidality, or serious mental-health distress: warm handoff to the platform's crisis-resource path documented in `creator-care-program-en.html` (and `creator-care-program-es.html`). Do not attempt to counsel; do refer.
- When in doubt reference the **Duty of Care** resources published by PNPtv! (`pnptv-duty-of-care-en.pdf` / `pnptv-duty-of-care-es.pdf`).

### 4.4. Punctuality on scheduled slots.

- See Section 3.8. Punctuality is a positive standard, not just an enforcement item.

### 4.5. Solidarity with other creators.

- Other creators are **colleagues, not competition**. Refer members when a request isn't your thing but is someone else's specialty. Signal-boost each other's promo. Celebrate each other's wins in the Partner Program Telegram group.
- Collab streams, cross-tips, and joint hangouts are actively encouraged.

### 4.6. Warm handoff for members in crisis.

- If a member appears to be in acute distress (medical, mental-health, safety, doxxing incident, or self-harm ideation): pause the interaction, direct them to the **PNPtv! Creator Care crisis resource path** (`/docs/creator-care-program-en.html`), and post to Slack `#creator-support` so ops and Cristina AI can follow up.
- You are not a crisis counselor. You are a first-line responder whose job is to interrupt harm and refer up.

### 4.7. Dignified representation of the PNP community.

- The community includes people newly experimenting, people in long-term maintenance use, people in recovery, people who are sober but stay connected to the culture, people whose relationship to PNP is complicated. Your public voice as a PNPtv! Creator should be inclusive of that range.
- Do not use PNPtv! as a platform to moralize at, shame, or "wake up" members. That is not the value proposition.

## 5. Enforcement Ladder

Consistent across all four Partner Program policy documents.

**5.1. Step 1 — Verbal coaching.** Informal Slack DM from your ops contact (Santino, Miguel, or designated moderator). Documented internally. No public record.

**5.2. Step 2 — Written warning.** Formal message in Slack `#ext-[handle]` naming the behavior, referencing the Code section, and stating the corrective expectation. Acknowledgment required within 72 hours. Kept on file.

**5.3. Step 3 — Suspension.** Platform access suspended. **Pending earnings are frozen** for the duration of the investigation (not confiscated — frozen). Investigation completed within 7 calendar days. Creator receives written notice of allegations and the opportunity to respond in writing (or by voice call if requested). Outcome: reinstatement (with any warranted corrective plan), extended suspension, or move to Step 4.

**5.4. Step 4 — Termination for cause.** Partner Program participation ended per Section 14 of the Partner Agreement (Document 2). Due process: written notice, 5 business days to respond, ops review, final written decision. Earnings owed for pre-termination activity are paid out per the Payout terms in the Partner Agreement, subject to any liquidated-damages or clawback offset under Document 7 (Anti-Poaching) or Document 2 for material breach.

**5.5. Progression.** Escalation is normally linear — you get warned before you get suspended — **except** for Section 2 zero-tolerance violations, which begin at Step 3 or Step 4 depending on severity, and repeated Anti-Poaching (Document 7) violations, which escalate per that document's schedule.

**5.6. Reset.** Warnings older than **12 months** with no repeat behavior in that time roll off the enforcement ladder for progression purposes. They remain in the file for context.

**5.7. Appeals.** Any decision at Step 2 or above can be appealed once to Santino (if not already the decision-maker) within 14 days. Appeal is on paper unless a call is requested.

## 6. Reporting a Violation

**6.1. If the alleged violator is a member.** Use the in-platform `/report` flow (fastest — routes to moderation queue) or post in Slack `#creator-support` if you need creator-side coordination.

**6.2. If the alleged violator is another creator.** Options in ascending formality:

- **(a)** DM directly to Santino or Miguel in Slack;
- **(b)** Email `report@pnptv.app` (routes to Santino);
- **(c)** Anonymous form at [ANONYMOUS-REPORT-FORM-URL — to be provisioned] — routed to Santino; identity of reporter is not disclosed to the alleged violator.

**6.3. If the alleged violator is a staff member (including Santino, Carlos, Miguel).**

- **(a)** Email `oversight@pnptv.app` — this address is monitored by an external adviser (independent counsel, TBD). Not read by the person being reported.
- **(b)** Or use the anonymous form above.

**6.4. No retaliation.** Retaliating against a good-faith reporter — reducing their tier, denying promotional slots, initiating pretextual warnings, hostile Slack comments, off-platform pressure — is itself a **Section 2.12 zero-tolerance violation**. This applies whether the reporter is a creator, member, or staff.

**6.5. Bad-faith reports.** Weaponizing the reporting system against another creator you don't like is a warning-ladder violation under Section 3.1 (drama) and, if repeated, escalates.
