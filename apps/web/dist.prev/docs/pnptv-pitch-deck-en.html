The Problem

Adult platforms are built to exploit, not protect.

A $100B+ global industry with no platform that treats its creators as human beings — and no platform that speaks to the PNP community at all.

  • 💸
    Creators are exploited on day one
    Most platforms take 20–50% with no transparency, no minimum guarantee, and no support infrastructure. Creators absorb all the risk while platforms capture most of the value.
  • 🏳️‍🌈
    The PNP community has zero dedicated platforms
    Gay and queer adults who are part of PNP culture — estimated tens of millions globally — are scattered across Grindr, OnlyFans, and Telegram with no community home. No content moderation calibrated to their reality. No harm reduction. No belonging.
  • 🆘
    No welfare infrastructure anywhere in the industry
    Creator burnout, coercion, and trafficking are industry-wide problems. No platform has built a systematic intake process, ongoing check-ins, or a distress protocol. The industry's answer is a ToS and a ban button.
  • 🔒
    Payment infrastructure penalizes adult creators
    Visa/Mastercard restrictions, sudden bans, and chargeback fraud destroy creator income overnight. Legacy platforms leave creators exposed to payment risk with no alternatives.

Technology

Production-grade infrastructure. Not a prototype.

Built to handle scale from day one — with architecture decisions made by engineers who've seen what breaks at volume.

⚛️

Frontend

React + Vite + Tailwind CSS. PWA with passkey authentication (phishing-resistant by design). Mobile-first. Zero dependence on Stripe or legacy payment UIs.

🖥️

Backend

Node.js / Express / PostgreSQL / Redis. BullMQ job queues for async operations. PostGIS for geolocation (Nearby feature). Socket.IO for real-time events.

🎥

Video

LiveKit for private video calls. RTMP/Restreamer for live broadcasts. Designed around the creator's workflow, not a generic video SDK.

🔒

Security

100% passwordless auth (passkeys + magic links). AES-256 payment data encryption. AI age verification via Azure Face API — photo discarded immediately after analysis, never stored.

₿

Payments

NowPayments: BTC, DASH, USDT TRC-20, USDT Base, Meru. No chargebacks. No bank deplatforming risk. Real-time fraud detection at transaction level.

🤖

AI (in production)

Cristina AI for 24/7 support. Grok-based content moderation calibrated for PNP context. AI content description generation for creator uploads. More agents on the roadmap.

Architecture roadmap in execution: BullMQ event bus (eliminating silent job failures), circuit breakers for third-party dependencies, OpenTelemetry observability, selective microservice extraction. This is an engineering team that knows what's next — not one that has to figure it out under pressure.

Competitive Position

Five layers of defensible advantage.

Each layer takes years to build and can't be purchased. Combined, they create a position that a VC-backed generalist platform cannot replicate.

  1. Community specificity
    PNP + queer niche = deep loyalty, near-zero substitution. Members cannot find what PNPtv! offers anywhere else. This isn't a feature — it's the foundation.
  2. Creator welfare brand
    A creator who feels protected stays and refers others. A creator who feels exploited leaves and tells everyone. Our welfare-first intake is a durable reputation moat that grows with every successful creator.
  3. Safety and harm reduction credibility
    Harm reduction built into the product — not bolted on for PR — becomes a legal and reputational shield. Regulators, journalists, and partners evaluate platforms on this. We're ahead by years.
  4. Technical reliability
    Modern architecture (BullMQ, circuit breakers, PostGIS, LiveKit) gives us better uptime and creator tools than any comparable platform. Our tech lead designed this to scale — not to launch.
  5. Community trust and founder relationships
    Santino's personal brand and decade of community trust is not replicable by any competitor. The community sees a founder, not a startup. That's a distribution advantage money can't buy.
