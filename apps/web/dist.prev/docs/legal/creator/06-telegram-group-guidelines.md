# PNPtv! Partner Program Telegram Group Guidelines

**Policy Document 6 in the Creator Partner Program legal package.** This is a policy, not a per-signature contract. Creators agree to it by joining the Partner Program Telegram group; the Partner Agreement (Document 2) incorporates it by reference.

---

## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

El grupo de Telegram del Programa de Socios es una **comunidad informal entre colegas** — no un canal de soporte, no una cola de tickets, no un substituto de Slack. Está diseñado para saludos, celebraciones, preguntas casuales, apoyo moral entre creadores y ocasionales avances de noticias de plataforma por parte de Santino o Miguel. Los temas de trabajo formal — pagos, quejas sobre miembros específicos, decisiones de plataforma — van a **Slack `#ext-[handle]`** (privado) o **`#creator-support`** (público-profesional), no aquí.

**No permitido**: drama, chismes sobre otros creadores, cazar suscriptores de otros creadores, PII de miembros, capturas de pantalla de miembros, promoción agresiva de plataformas competidoras, desnudos no solicitados. **Moderación**: Santino, Miguel, y 1–2 creadores rotativos como moderadores opcionales (con un pequeño estipendio mensual — monto por definir). **Escalada** consistente con el Código de Conducta (Documento 4). **Opt-out**: puedes salir del grupo de Telegram en cualquier momento sin perder tu participación en el Programa de Socios; Slack `#ext-[handle]` sigue siendo tu canal principal.

---

## 1. Purpose of the Group

**1.1.** The Partner Program Telegram group is an **informal peer community** for creators in the Partner Program. Behind-the-scenes chat, quick moral support, celebrating wins, sharing casual industry observations, occasional platform-news previews from Santino or Miguel.

**1.2.** It is explicitly **not**:

- a support-ticket queue;
- a substitute for Slack `#ext-[handle]` (private ops) or `#creator-support` (group ops);
- a place for member-facing communication;
- a channel for financial matters;
- a place for grievance-airing about other creators, members, or the platform.

**1.3.** The group exists because informal peer community is genuinely useful — creators feeling isolated is a known welfare risk in adult work, and a chat where colleagues can casually connect is a lightweight version of the guild culture the industry has historically lacked. That value depends on the group not becoming a drama channel. These Guidelines protect the value.

---

## 2. What Belongs in the Telegram Group

- **2.1.** Peer greetings, "good morning," birthday shout-outs, "you made it through Monday" energy.
- **2.2.** Wins — "just hit a new tip record," "first callback booking of the month," "someone renewed for the 12th consecutive month."
- **2.3.** Casual questions to other creators — "has anyone streamed on iPad recently?", "what mic is everyone using?", "is anyone else getting the login glitch this morning?" (bug reports move to Slack `#creator-support` — the Telegram note is fine for quick sanity-check).
- **2.4.** Behind-the-scenes photos of yourself only. Not members. Not other creators without their explicit consent. Studio setups, outfit prep, "look at this ridiculous rigging" content — all welcome.
- **2.5.** Sharing your **published** promo content (a link to a public teaser you posted on X, an announcement you're doing a special stream Friday) — signal boosts are actively encouraged.
- **2.6.** Community moments — mutual support during rough weeks, condolences, celebrations, occasional venting about universal creator experiences (bad lighting day, streaming service crash, general tiredness). Universal ≠ specific complaints about a person or the platform.
- **2.7.** Industry news and general observations — a policy change on another platform, a news story affecting adult creators, a security alert.
- **2.8.** Language — **English and Spanish are both welcome**. Use whichever you're comfortable with. Auto-translate is encouraged. Bilingual creators are welcome to translate spontaneously when helpful.

---

## 3. What Does NOT Belong in the Telegram Group

These do not belong in the Telegram group. They belong in the channels indicated.

- **3.1. Payout or financial disputes** → Slack `#ext-[handle]` (private with ops).
- **3.2. Bug reports and feature requests** → Slack `#creator-support` (so ops sees it and tracks it; Telegram note as a heads-up is fine but the formal report needs to be in Slack).
- **3.3. Complaints about specific members** → Slack `#ext-[handle]` (private). Never in the Telegram group where the complaint effectively becomes a broadcast.
- **3.4. Complaints about specific other creators** → nowhere in group chat. If there is a real concern, DM Santino or Miguel directly in Slack. Airing it in the Telegram group is drama (Document 4 § 3.1) and triggers the ladder.
- **3.5. Complaints about platform decisions** → Slack `#creator-support` (public but professional) if you want peer + ops discussion, or Slack DM to Santino / Miguel (private) if it's sensitive. Not in the Telegram group. The Telegram group is not the escalation path.
- **3.6. Member PII, screenshots of member DMs, member payment information, member real names, member locations, member medical status** — nowhere ever. Sharing here is both a Telegram Guidelines violation and an NDA (Document 1) breach and an outing violation (Document 4 § 2.5), and escalates fast.
- **3.7. Screenshots of the Telegram group itself, posted externally** — NDA breach. Do not.
- **3.8. Screenshots of internal Slack** posted to the Telegram group — inverse of the above, still an NDA issue.

## 4. Moderation Rules

The following are enforceable in the group per Section 6.

### 4.1. No drama, no gossip about other creators or members.

- See Document 4 § 3.1 through 3.4. All of it applies here specifically.
- Subtweeting via Telegram ("someone in this group thinks they're the only one who...") is drama.

### 4.2. No poaching.

- Do not use the group to identify or contact other creators' known regulars, do not offer discounts pitched at another creator's member base, do not use the group's visibility into who's active/inactive to time your own outreach. See Document 7 (Anti-Poaching).

### 4.3. No off-platform solicitation of other creators' members via the group.

- Also Document 7 territory. Especially escalates when using group-derived intel to do it.

### 4.4. No promotional links to competitor platforms.

- Mentioning that you have a presence on other platforms in a self-introduction (`!platforms` command triggers a template listing your handles across services — set up during onboarding) is fine.
- Posting "hey come to my [other platform] instead" is not. Neither is repeatedly reposting your other-platform promo in the group when it is not context-appropriate.
- Sharing other-platform industry news (a policy change, a payout change, a shutdown) is fine — that's information.

### 4.5. Consent among creators — no unsolicited nudes.

- Even in a group of adult creators, consent matters.
- Behind-the-scenes / obviously-promo shots of published content (a still from a stream you did last night, an outfit reveal) are welcome.
- Random full-nude sends without context, sexually explicit DMs to individual creators through the group DM feature without consent — not welcome. It is sexual harassment in a professional space and triggers Document 4 § 2.7.

### 4.6. No hate speech.

- Same standard as Document 4 § 2.6. Slurs, dehumanizing framing, targeting protected classes — same zero-tolerance line.

### 4.7. No coordinated targeting of individuals.

- Do not use the group to organize a pile-on against a member, another creator, staff, or an outside individual (a competitor creator on another platform, a journalist, an anti-adult activist). Individual creators making individual statements is fine; coordinating a group action against a person is harassment.

### 4.8. Language, tone, humor.

- Adult, queer, PNP-community-inflected humor is expected and welcome. This is not a corporate Slack.
- The line is the Document 4 § 2 standard — anything that would be a zero-tolerance violation there is a zero-tolerance violation here.

## 5. Moderators

**5.1. Composition.**

- **Santino** — permanent moderator, founder.
- **Miguel** — permanent moderator.
- **1–2 rotating creator moderators** — selected by opt-in from active Partner Program creators in good standing, on rotating terms (proposed: 6-month rotation, up to 2 consecutive terms).

**5.2. Compensation for creator moderators.**

- Small monthly stipend, amount TBD (working proposal: USD $150/month; open for review by ops).
- Stipend is separate from Partner Program earnings and does not affect the 70/30 revenue split.
- Paid via the standard payout channel per Partner Agreement Section 8.

**5.3. Moderator duties.**

- Enforce Sections 2, 3, and 4 above.
- Escalate ambiguous cases to Santino or Miguel.
- Welcome new members joining the group.
- Log serious incidents to Slack `#creator-support` (redacted where PII is involved) so ops has an audit trail.

**5.4. Moderator conduct standard.**

- Moderators are held to the same Code of Conduct (Document 4) as all creators, and additionally to a higher bar on Section 3.1 (no drama) given their role.

## 6. Enforcement in the Telegram Group

Consistent with the Document 4 ladder, tailored to the Telegram context.

**6.1. Step 1 — Verbal coaching.** Private DM from a moderator to the creator, referencing the section of these Guidelines the behavior violated.

**6.2. Step 2 — Temporary mute (7 days).** Creator can view the group but cannot post for 7 days. Moderator posts a brief neutral note ("[handle] is muted for 7 days per Telegram Guidelines § [X]"). No shaming detail.

**6.3. Step 3 — Removal from the Telegram group.** Creator loses access to the Telegram group. **Partner Program participation continues** on a Slack-only basis. Slack `#ext-[handle]` remains open. Discovery placements, payouts, and platform access are unaffected.

**6.4. Step 4 — Termination for cause under Document 2 § 14.** Only reached if the pattern in the Telegram group is part of a broader Code of Conduct pattern that has already escalated in Slack per Document 4 § 5.

**6.5. Reset.** Warnings and mutes older than **6 months** with no repeat behavior roll off for progression purposes (note: shorter reset than the Code of Conduct's 12-month reset, because the Telegram context is more informal and easier to correct).

**6.6. Zero-tolerance items.** For zero-tolerance violations under Document 4 § 2 that surface first in the Telegram group (e.g., a hate-speech incident, an outing incident), moderators go straight to Step 3 (removal) and Slack `#ext-[handle]` opens the Document 4 § 2 investigation immediately.

**6.7. Appeals.** A muted or removed creator may appeal once to Santino within 14 days. Appeal is on paper unless a call is requested.

## 7. Opt-out

**7.1.** A creator may **leave the Telegram group at any time without losing Partner Program participation**. Slack `#ext-[handle]` remains the primary and canonical channel regardless.

**7.2.** Some creators simply do not want another chat in their life. That is respected. Ops will not read anything into it.

**7.3.** A creator who opts out and later wants back in can request re-invite in Slack `#ext-[handle]`. Re-invite is granted unless the creator was previously removed at Step 3 in the current 12-month window.

**7.4.** Notification opt-in.

- Group messages are muted by default at Telegram's client level. You control whether to receive push notifications.
- Ops will not judge whether or how frequently you read.

---

## 8. Data and Retention

**8.1.** Telegram group message history is retained by Telegram per Telegram's own retention practices. PNPtv! does not maintain a separate archive of group content.

**8.2.** Incident logs — when a moderator escalates a specific incident to `#creator-support` per § 5.3, the summary of that incident is retained in Slack per Slack's standard retention (currently: PNPtv!'s Slack workspace retains messages indefinitely; this is subject to change and will be announced in `#creator-announcements`).

**8.3.** PII in the group. Do not share member PII in the group (Section 3.6). If PII leaks accidentally, notify a moderator and it will be deleted from the group promptly.

---

## 9. Scope and Amendment

**9.1.** These Guidelines bind all members of the Partner Program Telegram group. Continued membership after an amendment constitutes acceptance.

**9.2.** PNPtv! may amend these Guidelines with **14 days' notice** posted in the group itself and in Slack `#creator-announcements`.

**9.3.** These Guidelines are subordinate to the Code of Conduct (Document 4) and the Communications Policy (Document 5) — where a conflict exists, those documents control. This document controls only Telegram-group-specific matters (moderator structure, mute mechanics, opt-out).
