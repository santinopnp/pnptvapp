## Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)

Este Acuerdo del Programa de Socios Creadores es un **contrato de prestación de servicios** (no laboral) entre PNPtv! y el Creador. El Creador es un **contratista independiente** responsable de sus propios impuestos, aportes a seguridad social y afiliaciones. PNPtv! otorga al Creador acceso a la plataforma y el derecho a monetizar mediante suscripciones, propinas, llamadas privadas, hangouts y contenido VOD. El **reparto de ingresos** es **70% para el Creador y 30% para PNPtv!** sobre los ingresos netos (después de comisiones del procesador de pagos). Los **umbrales de pago**: mínimo **USD $100** para lote semanal y mínimo **USD $50** para retiro a demanda; los tokens regalados **nunca acumulan** ganancias. La moneda interna es **Ru$h** a razón de **1 USD = 6 Ru$h** (base). Todo Creador debe completar la **verificación 2257** (identificación oficial verificada por IA + revisión humana) antes de ganar. El plazo inicial es **12 meses con renovación mensual automática**; cualquier parte puede terminar con **30 días de aviso**; PNPtv! puede terminar de inmediato por causa justa. Se rige por la ley colombiana; disputas por **arbitraje en la Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá D.C.**

## 5. Commercial Terms — Revenue Split, Rate, Currency

**5.1. Revenue Split.** For all Net Revenue attributable to the Creator's Content or services, the Creator is entitled to **seventy percent (70%)** and PNPtv! retains **thirty percent (30%)**. Payment-processor fees are deducted from gross revenue **before** the 70/30 split (i.e., both Parties share proportionally in processor cost).

**5.2. Ru$h Token Rate.** The internal currency is **Ru$h**. The base rate is **1 USD = 6 Ru$h**. Package bonus tiers (e.g., pkg_50 = 315 Ru$h, pkg_100 = 648 Ru$h, pkg_500 = 3,360 Ru$h) may apply as posted from time to time. When Creator earnings are settled from Ru$h to USD, the base rate applies unless a superseding rate is displayed on the platform at the time of the transaction.

**5.3. Gifted Tokens Do Not Accrue.** Gifted Tokens spent on the Creator's Content or services **do not** generate Creator earnings. Only purchased Ru$h tokens (i.e., tokens acquired by members with fiat or crypto) generate Creator earnings. This rule is enforced in the platform's token ledger service and is not subject to case-by-case override. **Exception:** as of the Effective Date, Gifted Tokens may be spent on live tips to Santino and Lex only, pursuant to a separate internal policy; that exception does not create Creator earnings for any other Creator.

**5.4. Payment Rail.** PNPtv!'s active payment provider is **NowPayments** (crypto). Member payments are received primarily in USDT, USDC, and other cryptocurrencies supported by NowPayments. Additional providers may be added by PNPtv! from time to time; the Creator will be notified of any material change via the Communications Policy channels.

## 6. Payout Mechanics

**6.1. Weekly Batch Payouts.** PNPtv! processes a scheduled weekly Payout batch on a day designated by PNPtv! (as posted on the platform; currently under review by operations). To be included in a weekly batch, the Creator's accrued balance must equal or exceed **USD $100** at the batch cutoff. Balances below the threshold roll forward.

**6.2. On-Demand Cashouts.** The Creator may request a Payout at any time, subject to a minimum of **USD $50** per request. PNPtv! aims to process on-demand cashouts within five (5) business days but does not guarantee any specific timing given crypto-settlement and compliance-review windows.

**6.3. Payout Method.** Payouts are made in one of the following methods, at the Creator's election (subject to feasibility in the Creator's jurisdiction):

- **(a)** **Crypto** — USDT or USDC preferred (either TRC-20 or ERC-20, at PNPtv!'s current supported networks); other cryptocurrencies may be supported from time to time; or
- **(b)** **Bank transfer** — where feasible in the Creator's jurisdiction and where the Creator has provided complete, accurate banking details and any KYC information required by the payment intermediary.

**6.4. Holds and Chargebacks.** PNPtv! may hold Payouts pending:

- **(a)** verification of the Creator's 2257 records and current standing (see Section 7);
- **(b)** investigation of suspected fraud, chargeback risk, or Code of Conduct breach;
- **(c)** resolution of chargebacks or reversals; the Creator's share of any chargeback shall be **deducted from future Payouts** or invoiced if no future Payouts exist; and
- **(d)** compliance with tax withholding or reporting obligations.

**6.5. Fees.** PNPtv! does not charge a Payout fee beyond payment-processor and network fees actually incurred, which will be transparently disclosed on the Creator's earnings dashboard.

**6.6. Currency Fluctuation.** For crypto Payouts, the Creator bears the risk of any price movement between the time earnings accrue in USD-equivalent terms and the time of Payout. PNPtv! makes no representation as to the future value of any cryptocurrency.

## 7. 2257 Compliance

**7.1.** The Creator represents, warrants, and covenants that:

- **(a)** the Creator is at least **eighteen (18) years of age** and was at least 18 years of age at the time any Content depicting the Creator was produced;
- **(b)** any other person appearing in the Creator's Content was at least 18 years of age at the time of production, was competent to consent, and did in fact consent to being depicted and to the publication of the Content on the platform;
- **(c)** the Creator has provided PNPtv! with valid government-issued photo identification for the Creator, and shall provide the same for each other performer, in advance of any monetization; and
- **(d)** the Creator will maintain, and provide to PNPtv! on request, all records required under **18 U.S.C. § 2257** and **28 C.F.R. Part 75** for content in which the Creator is a **secondary producer** or **primary producer**.

**7.2. AI-Assisted Verification.** PNPtv! operates an AI-assisted 2257 verification flow (currently powered by the xAI grok-4.3 vision model with human admin review). The Creator consents to the automated extraction of identification-document fields for the purpose of verification and to the storage of those fields and the source image by PNPtv!'s designated Custodian.

**7.3. Custodian of Records.** PNPtv! designates a **Custodian of Records** under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. § 75.4, whose identity and business address are posted on the platform's 2257 Compliance Notice. The Custodian's records are maintained at PNPtv!'s designated premises and made available for inspection in accordance with US federal law.

**7.4. No Monetization Without Verification.** The Creator understands and agrees that **no monetization** (Payouts, tips, subscriptions, tokens) will be released until 2257 verification is complete and the Creator's 2257 record is in good standing. If 2257 records lapse or become non-compliant, PNPtv! may suspend monetization until re-verification.

## 13. Term and Termination

**13.1. Initial Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date (last-Party signature date) and continues for an initial term of **twelve (12) months** (the "**Initial Term**").

**13.2. Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive **monthly** periods unless terminated in accordance with this Section 13.

**13.3. Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement, with or without cause, on **thirty (30) days' prior written notice** to the other Party via the Communications Policy channels.

**13.4. Termination for Cause by PNPtv!.** PNPtv! may terminate this Agreement **immediately** upon written notice to the Creator for any of the following causes:

- **(a)** breach of any 2257 obligation under Section 7;
- **(b)** publication or attempted publication of Content in violation of any absolute prohibition in Section 8.2(b);
- **(c)** material breach of the Code of Conduct, the Communications Policy, or the Content Standards;
- **(d)** fraud, chargeback abuse, or attempted fraud against PNPtv! or its members;
- **(e)** conviction (or plea equivalent) for any crime involving moral turpitude, fraud, or sexual offenses;
- **(f)** material breach of the NDA (Document 1); or
- **(g)** failure to remedy a curable material breach of this Agreement within ten (10) business days after written notice specifying the breach.

**13.5. Termination for Cause by the Creator.** The Creator may terminate this Agreement **immediately** upon written notice to PNPtv! for **material non-payment** of Payouts (i.e., failure to pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days of the applicable Payout date) that PNPtv! fails to cure within ten (10) business days after written notice.

**13.6. Effect of Termination.**

- **(a)** **Content availability window.** For **ninety (90) days** following termination, the Creator retains access to a data-export tool to download the Creator's own uploaded Content (source files where feasible; otherwise best-available master).
- **(b)** **Post-termination Payout.** Revenue accrued through the effective date of termination, net of any offsets (including chargebacks and refunds properly attributable to the Creator's Content), shall be paid to the Creator within **thirty (30) days** of the effective date of termination, subject to Payout thresholds being met (or, if not met, on a final "final settlement" basis to be documented).
- **(c)** **Content license tail.** The post-termination license window for promotional use of the Creator's Content is governed by the Content License (Document 3).
- **(d)** **Survival.** Sections 5.3, 6.4, 6.6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 13.6, 13.7, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19, together with any provision that by its nature is intended to survive, shall survive termination.

**13.7. Impact on Member Subscribers Upon Creator Account Termination.**

The Creator acknowledges that members of the platform ("Subscribers") may hold active Creator Subscriptions at the time of termination, and the following applies:

- **(a)** **Voluntary termination or departure.** Upon any voluntary termination (Section 13.3) or voluntary account deletion, all active Creator Subscriptions to the Creator are cancelled effective the date of departure. The prorated value of each Subscriber's remaining subscription period is credited to that Subscriber's platform balance as Ru$h tokens (at the applicable base rate of 1 USD = 6 Ru$h). These credits are funded from the Creator's accrued but unpaid Net Revenue balance where sufficient; any shortfall is covered by the Company. The Creator acknowledges and consents to this offset mechanism.

- **(b)** **Termination for cause — non-egregious.** Upon termination for cause under Section 13.4 for non-egregious violations (excluding 13.4(a), (b), (c)), active Creator Subscriptions are cancelled and Subscriber credits are issued. Subscriber credit costs are offset against the Creator's post-termination Payout under Section 13.6(b).

- **(c)** **Termination for egregious cause.** Upon termination for any of the egregious violations listed in Section 13.4(a), (b), or (c) (CSAM, fraud, non-consensual recordings, identity fraud), all pending Payouts are withheld indefinitely pending investigation and any applicable law enforcement referral. Subscriber credits are funded entirely by the Company. The Creator forfeits any claim to withheld Payouts if criminal proceedings are initiated.

- **(d)** **Temporary suspension exceeding 7 days.** If the Creator's account is suspended for more than 7 days and Subscribers request credit extensions, those extension costs may be offset from the Creator's next Payout following reinstatement.

- **(e)** **No double payment.** In no event shall the Creator receive both a full post-termination Payout and retain Subscriber subscription fees for a period during which Subscribers did not have access to the Creator's Content. The parties agree that the offset mechanism in (a) and (b) above is the fair remedy.
